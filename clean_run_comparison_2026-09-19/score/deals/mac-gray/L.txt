Account | Mac-Gray, a laundry facilities management company, ran one sale process. After the Board authorized exploration in April 2013 and its banker approached Party A, an unsolicited Party A proposal in June ($17.00-$19.00) led the Special Committee to solicit 50 parties (15 strategic, 35 financial) on June 24; 20 signed confidentiality agreements and four bid seriously: CSC/Pamplona and Party A (both strategic), and financial bidders Party B and Party C. Round 1 indications (due July 23) were judged low; the four advanced to management meetings and revised indications (due September 9), then to final indications (due September 18). CSC/Pamplona led at every stage ($18.50, $19.50, $20.75), Party B put the highest headline value on September 18 ($21.50 per share as valued, cash plus options), and Party C dropped out at the final deadline. On September 19 the Special Committee chose CSC/Pamplona, secured $21.25 per share with exclusivity on September 24, and signed the merger agreement on October 14, 2013, announced October 15.
