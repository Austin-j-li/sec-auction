1. Commercial account
Story | After a weak 05/21/2014 earnings release and public pressure from JANA Partners (9.9% Schedule 13D on 07/03/2014) and Longview (07/07/2014 letter advocating a sale), the PetSmart board decided on 08/13/2014 to explore strategic alternatives including a possible sale, publicly announced the exploration on 08/19/2014, and excluded antitrust-risky Industry Participant from the process. J.P. Morgan was contacted by 27 potential participants (3 strategic, 24 financial); 15 financial buyers signed confidentiality and standstill agreements in the first week of October 2014 and received uniform diligence access, management presentations and Profit Improvement Plan information.
Six parties submitted non-binding indications of interest on 10/30/2014 (Buyer Group $81.00–$83.00; another bidder $80.00–$85.00; Bidder 2 $78.00 increased to $81.00–$84.00; an unnamed bidder whose range reached at least $80.00; and two submitters below $80.00). The board advanced the four at or above $80.00 to the final round on 11/03/2014; two of them combined as "Bidder 3" (units 4 → 3: Buyer Group, Bidder 2, Bidder 3).
Final bids on 12/10/2014 were $80.70 (Buyer Group) and $80.35 (Bidder 2), with Bidder 3 giving only an uncompetitive verbal indication (not above approximately $78.00). The ad hoc committee extended bidding to 12/12/2014, when Bidder 2 confirmed $81.50 as best and final and the Buyer Group moved $82.50 → $83.00 best and final with committed financing and substantially executable documentation.
The board approved the Buyer Group merger on 12/13/2014 and the parties signed on 12/14/2014 at $83.00 per share in cash — a 38.8% premium to the 07/02/2014 unaffected price and 6.9% above the 12/12/2014 close of $77.67 — with Longview rolling over 3,012,050 shares (about $250m) and voting 7,424,591 shares in favour. Observed outcome: signed with the Buyer Group; no post-signing competing proposal as of 02/02/2015; closing not observed in the filing (expected first half of 2015).
Initiation assessment | Mixed, with activist influence and substantial inbound interest: the board’s own alternatives review and Profit Improvement Plan planning (06/18/2014) preceded, but did not cause, a sale; the 08/13/2014 sale decision followed the JANA/Longview campaigns, and the process then ran largely on parties approaching J.P. Morgan after the 08/19/2014 publicity.
