## Reconciliations applied

Items 1–15 are the specified reconciliations in DRAFTING_SPEC §4; 16–19 record additional wording reconciliations identified during integration. Items 1, 16 and 17 are superseded by the 28 September rulings; each now states the current text first and keeps its earlier resolution for the record.

1. **Round 1 dating (superseded by 28 September ruling 4).** Earlier text: E6 L189 used the launching board decision only for outreach dated within the following week, and undated outreach used the sale-process board meeting immediately before the first NDAs. After ruling 4: E6 [L189](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L189) dates round 1 at the launching decision when the outreach is dated within a week of it, counting outreach dated only to a period from the period's start; undated outreach uses the board meeting on the sale process immediately before the first NDAs or, if there is none, the latest dated event the filing places before the outreach. The bilateral fallback still applies only when no wider outreach occurs. The PetSmart date is unchanged; Penford (August 28, 2014) and Synacor (May 8, 2018; August 25, 2019; July 13, 2020) get single dates instead of derived ranges (ROUND_MAP). Ruling 9 folds the undated-outreach text into one default, the latest dated sale-process event before the first NDAs the outreach produced, or else before the outreach; PetSmart, Synacor P1 and P2 keep their dates.
2. **A stage's first final request:** E6 L193 and L199 treat the first offer request as carrying out the admission stage, with its finality supplied by that request. Trigger (b) opens a round after the current stage's offers are received or due. Example 6 illustrates this without filing text.
3. **A later request to fewer admitted parties:** E6 L197 opens a round on a later common request omitting some admitted parties even before the stage has received offers. The final-round exception remains. Datalink's July 27 and August 16 stages remain distinct; PetSmart's December extension does not open another.
4. **Documents at another time and a retained markup:** E11 L273 puts the F3 carry-forward exception in the same paragraph as F1's timing restriction.
5. **Definitive negotiation:** E10 L259 defines it once; E6 (c), E10 reaffirmations and E11 route 3 refer to it. It covers the target's definitive-negotiation decision, winner selection or executed exclusivity.
6. **Silent Formal bids:** E12 L285 retains Not stated for silent individual columns. L303 permits None only for the summary under the exact Formal-bid tests, no H trigger and no Required exclusivity.
7. **Exclusivity and Conditions:** A.3 L16 and E12 L303–307 express the same one-way relationship. Required exclusivity makes Conditions at least Light and never supplies Heavy by itself.
8. **Invited bidder in final round:** E11 L275 covers every bid during that round, including a late answer or unrequested revision. This is confirmed by evening ruling 3, not an unresolved drafter proposal.
9. **Withdrawal and re-entry:** E11 L279 ends the old markup and final invitation at withdrawal; fresh qualification is necessary. This prevents the earlier WDC qualification from carrying into its June 10 return. Ruling 9 cuts the sentence that referencing old terms is not a new markup: route 1 needs a markup submitted with the bid, so WDC's June 10 statement that it “was otherwise prepared to move forward on the transaction terms previously proposed on May 28, 2013” (sTec p.32) is still Informal.
10. **Document submission with a material change:** E10 L265 gives one Bid row under the revision/condition rules, with a blank price unless restated; the unchanged first submission alone receives the F2 copied-price reaffirmation. Later changed submissions follow the same one-row rule. This preserves the June 20 standstill anchor.
11. **Decision while rival exclusivity remains:** E6 (d) L203 dates the opening at the first renewed approach after exclusivity ends, not the earlier decision to approach. This preserves Datalink's October 1 anchor. Ruling 9 cuts the explicit sentence: (d) is met only by an approach after exclusivity ends, and an admission decision after a round announced as final opens nothing (E6 L193), so the September 29 decision still opens nothing.
12. **Economic scope:** E1 L143 carries the already-settled pre-separation scope ruling into the text. Buying every share of a remainder company is not, by itself, whole-company scope. This is not a new research decision.
13. **Renewed interest outreach:** E6 (d) L203 includes the first renewed approach after exclusivity, whether or not it already asks for an offer; carry-out steps stay within that reopening. This preserves Synacor's October 27 anchor and excludes December 30 as another opening.
14. **Bidder's own offered ceiling or range:** E10 L267 distinguishes it from a stock-price citation. E13 L311 clarified that a ceiling stated as a range retains both stated figures and the ceiling wording; a single stated figure still uses the applicable one-sided cell. Ruling 9 cuts that clarification: “A bidder's own range fills its endpoints” still gives Penford A's October 4 17.50–18.00, and only the Note's ceiling wording is no longer required.
15. **Considered, unchanged:** Other-scope bid rows retain Formality and Conditions; the process Question stays required even when defaults decide the map; all nine exit reasons remain (see below).
16. **Financing precedence (superseded by 28 September ruling 3).** E12 [L288](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L288) no longer keeps the precedence clause: where the filing says the bid lacks a firm or committed financing arrangement, Financing is Contingent even if the bid is stated not to be subject to a financing condition. Earlier text, for the record: E12 L288 stated explicitly that a bid expressly not subject to a financing condition is Committed even where financing documents lack firm commitment. That carried forward V114_SPEC D5, which lane_C_decisions §3 had preserved (“F6 must not override it”); ruling 3 overturns it.
17. **Missed deadline versus omitted invitation (superseded by 28 September rulings 1, 5, 6 and 7).** E14 closing event 3 ([L332](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L332)) now gives the ruling's exit: a bidder asked to bid by a due date that has not bid by then exits at the due date (Did not submit), whatever it says about still considering. The paragraph at [L336](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L336) gives, once for events 1 and 3, when such an exit does not occur (the target considers a bid from the bidder, admits it to a stage, gives it more time or asks it for an offer in time); a later return is Re-entered ([L338](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L338)). D2 [L93](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L93) and D3 [L117](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L117) lose the missed-due-date items, and E14 closing event 3 ([L332](SEC_Deal_Ledger_Extraction_Instruction.md#L332)) no longer requires the bidder to be “never mentioned again”. Earlier text, for the record: E14 L326 retained V114 D10's no-exit rule for a bidder that actually continues, while L330 closes a bidder omitted from the next stage even if it says it is still considering. This reproduced the stated Datalink outcome and the late-response rule, and the broader reading of the Stage-questions quotation was flagged for Austin; ruling 1 settled it.


18. **Timing of final announcement and route 2:** E11 L275 applies at the bid communication, as Part B requires. Thus a stage opened earlier by an admission decision can receive Announced as final on its Rounds line without retroactively changing earlier bids. All invited-bidder improvements, late answers and unsolicited revisions after the final announcement still qualify. This resolves the independent review's timing collision. Settled: BUILD_REVIEW §2 keeps it as drafted under evening ruling 3 and F1 (DECISIONS.md).
19. **Latest stated price after an intervening blank-price Bid:** E10 L265 uses the latest offer stating the current price as the “Same as #n” antecedent (the branch for an offer with no stated price is cut by ruling 9; no case in the nine deals used it). The adopted F2 decision-table text ([DECISIONS.md L43](../DECISIONS.md#L43)) says “copies the latest price”; the full record ([DECISIONS.md L191](../DECISIONS.md#L191)) calls it the latest offer. This clarification resolves those wordings when a blank-price commitment row intervenes; a commitment-only row does not erase that standing price (the preserved root HANDOFF commitment-change ruling). Conditions are still coded from the current document-submission evidence. This gives the copied-price analysis an actual price-bearing antecedent.
