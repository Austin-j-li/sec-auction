## Derived alternatives and acceptance limits

1. **Penford R1 precision.** Preferred: first contacts September 1–9. Alternative August 28 requires interpreting the loosely dated contacts as within seven days. The filing does not demonstrate that premise. This is a derive-cell question, not an anchor failure.
2. **Synacor early R1 dates.** Preferred: broad-outreach bounds above. Alternatives are the older first-NDA dates (January 10, 2018 and March 2019), but the draft allows that fallback only where the target never contacts other buyers; both processes later do. No dated sale-board meeting is provided to replace the bounds. P3 July 13 is a plausible exact-date reading of “also then”; preserving mid-July with July 13 lower bound avoids adding precision. Earlier partial-only and merger-of-equals activity does not add processes.
3. **Synacor January 6.** The draft literally produces three rounds in P3 because a September 23 inferred-final round already occurred. Treating finality as reset by October 27 would instead create R4 at January 6, inferred final. The current text supplies no reset rule. Both readings preserve the fixed October 27/December 30 anchor; the extra derived round needs Austin's decision. No new reset convention was added to the instruction or assumed here.
4. **Penford A's participation spells.** Literal E14 creates an October 3 noninvitation exit and October 4 re-entry, followed by the fixed October 14 final exit. A reading that leaves A continuously live to October 14 would need to treat the target's continued market-check interest as admission/continuation despite A's noninvitation to definitive negotiation. The three required prices, Informal classification and final signing exit are preserved either way; the live-count path is flagged.
5. **Deadline/exit bounds.** The Mac-Gray September 21 revision's status as a late required response versus a timely bidder's improvement, Providence's unnamed latest late arrival and Datalink C's October 20 versus October 26 exclusion require Review items. A due date or workbook rough date does not resolve missing filing precision.

Every §7 fixed process/round and retest anchor is represented.
