## Deadline and participation cross-check

These are the E9/E14 checks used to test the map; they are not substitute ledger rows. Later feedback alone is not enforcement, a due date is not proof of receipt, and an exit inferred from noninvitation precedes a later reported refusal. Ruling 1 (E14) applies throughout: a bidder asked to bid by a due date that has not bid by then exits at the due date (Did not submit), whatever it says about still considering; a bidder that bids late and is taken before the next round opens, or advanced to it, never left, and the due date's outcome is Extended (late bid accepted).
