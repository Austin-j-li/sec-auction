## 1. Convention meetings, 3–6 April 2016 (review; model judgment, 0.76) — **no change**

The five introductory meetings at the 2016 Connections Convention are not a new **Contact**.
GHF had already contacted 11 potential strategic buyers, G&W among them, during the week of
28 March (p. 28); that step is recorded as the cohort row #6 (with Party A as #1). C9 asks for
*first* contacts; C2 refuses rows for "routine calls, meetings, visits and document exchanges,
including every contact with a bidder already under NDA", and C3 forbids counting the same
party's same step twice. Nor is this a C2 information-access difference: it is a series of
introductory meetings at an industry convention, not information the target gave to some live
bidders and withheld from others. At most it is Note material, and the task says not to add rows
the instruction would fold into a note.

## 2. #46 (sheet row 47), Price low $24.00 (review; model judgment, 0.56) — **no change**

#46 is a **Bid reaffirmed** row, and the finding itself states the expectation: the row carries an
earlier price forward. C12 requires exactly that — the row "carries the standing price
('carried from #n')" and quotes the bidder's reaffirming act, here B's counsel returning a
revised merger agreement on 4 August (p. 31). The Note already says "Carried from #24", and
#24 is B's $24.00 LOI (p. 29). Price and quote are consistent with the convention.
