# Independent grade of `stec.xlsx`

I used only the supplied instruction and filing. I read the instruction in full, read the entire “Background of the Merger” on printed pp. 23–35, and wrote an independent timeline before opening the workbook. I then examined all 63 ledger rows and every populated field on all four sheets, checked the background again for omissions, and consulted relevant passages elsewhere in the filing. Row references below mean the ledger’s **#**, not the Excel worksheet row. The workbook was not edited.

## B. Findings, most important first

### B1. WDC’s amendment is coded as a new NDA execution — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** #21, Event, When and date bounds: `NDA signed`, exactly 04/17/2013. The Note correctly calls the document an addendum, but the coded event says a confidentiality agreement was signed that day.
- **Filing:** “Also on April 17, 2013, sTec entered into an addendum to its existing non-disclosure agreement with WDC.” (p. 28).
- **Rule:** C9 excludes “drafts, amendments” from NDA events. C1 permits expressly reused agreements to count for the auction screen; C10 prohibits inventing an execution date.
- **Proposed change:** Replace #21 with an `Other material event` on 04/17 documenting reuse of the existing NDA through an addendum. Its Note should establish participation under the existing agreement without claiming that the original agreement was executed that day; the original execution is known only to have occurred by 04/17. Do not add another NDA execution for the amendment. Keep the auction screen **Met, six independent counterparties**; changing the event label does not reduce the screen to five.

### B2. Heavy conditions are unsupported for two preliminary bids — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** #30, WDC 05/03, and #38, H 05/15: `Conditions = Heavy`. Both Notes acknowledge management presentations but rely on the absence of data-room access.
- **Filing:** “On April 18, 2013, members of our management held a meeting with representatives of WDC, at which our management provided a presentation on sTec” (p. 28). “On May 10, 2013, sTec held a management presentation with Company H.” (p. 29). H then submits a “written non-binding indication of interest” on 05/15 (p. 29); the WDC 05/03 bid passage likewise states the offer and cash range, without a financing or further substantive-diligence condition.
- **Rule:** C14 expressly defines substantive access as “a data room or management access.” It also states that “‘Non-binding’, or a price range, does not alone establish Heavy.”
- **Proposed change:** Use **Unclear** for these two bids unless a contemporaneous, affirmative condition can be identified in the supplied filing. Keep their prices, cash labels and Informal classification. Do not infer Light or None from silence either. This finding does not extend to D, whose continuing substantive diligence requirements are reported.

### B3. B’s response is a second first-contact event with an unsupported date window — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** #6 records B’s initiating `Bidder interest`; #7 adds `Contact`, Count 1, “approximately two weeks after 02/14/2013,” with bounds 02/21–03/07 and Sort date 02/28.
- **Filing:** “The company forwarded to the President of Company B a presentation on sTec’s technology and overall business. Approximately two weeks later, the President of Company B contacted Mr. Manouch Moshayedi” (p. 26). B then says it is not interested and can develop the technology independently. The presentation’s date is not specified as 02/14.
- **Rule:** C9 says an initiating Bidder interest row carries the first contact “without a duplicate Contact row,” and a declined initial enquiry is recorded with its response. C2 requires a small ledger. C10 does not authorize a fabricated plus-or-minus-one-week interval around an assumed presentation date.
- **Proposed change:** Fold B’s response and its stated reason into #6 and remove #7. Preserve “approximately two weeks after the presentation” in prose; do not turn 02/14 into a reported presentation date. No exit row is needed because B never demonstrably entered bidding.

### B4. H’s live status between advancement and refusal needs a Question — AMBIGUOUS

- **Location / workbook:** #40 says only WDC/D advance on 05/16; #41 says H was not admitted to round 2, yet #42 closes H only on 05/23 as `Did not submit`. Rounds says H was “kept alive.” No Question addresses this treatment.
- **Filing:** “BofA Merrill Lynch sent final round process letters and a draft merger agreement to WDC and Company D” (p. 30). H’s range “was not sufficient to move them forward in the process,” but H “could submit a revised indication of interest, which would be considered by the special committee” (p. 30). On 05/23, H “remained interested” but “was not able to increase its indicated value range” (p. 30).
- **Rule:** C16 closes a participant at the first named advancement set that excludes it and distinguishes target exclusion from non-submission. Part D requires a Question when continued participation changes live-bidder counts.
- **Proposed change:** Add a Question covering #40–#42 and Rounds. My preferred coding is an inferred `Dropped by target` by the 05/16 advancement, with the later refusal described separately or in the Note; an inferred exit uses `Inferred = Y` and `Exit reason = Not stated`. The alternative is to treat the express opportunity to improve as keeping H’s initial-stage participation open until 05/23, retaining the current close and its correctly captured `Would not improve earlier offer` reason. This is a **material unresolved interpretation, not a proven factual error**.

### B5. The quotation on WDC’s re-entry does not establish that event’s day — MINOR

- **Location / workbook:** #55 correctly records re-entry on 06/10 before the new bid, but quotes discussions “Over the period from June 1 to June 10, 2013.”
- **Filing:** “On June 10, 2013, WDC submitted a revised written indication of interest to acquire the company” (p. 32). This supports a return to bidding on the coded day; discussions while reconsidering interest do not independently do so.
- **Rule:** Part D requires the quotation to support the row’s specific claim.
- **Proposed change:** Replace #55’s quotation with the dated submission passage on p. 32. Keep the event, date, Count 1, reference to exit #52, and position before #56. This is an evidence-selection correction, not a missing re-entry or a wrong substantive date.

### B6. Filing date and proxy-statement date need to be distinguished — MINOR

- **Location / workbook:** Deal facts, `Filing type and date`: “DEFM14A, proxy statement dated 08/07/2013.”
- **Filing / supplied source:** “The proxy statement is dated August 7, 2013” (unnumbered shareholder-letter page). The supplied filename is `stec_2013-08-08_DEFM14A.htm`.
- **Rule:** B5 requests the filing type and date; A prohibits filling evidence gaps with invented dates.
- **Proposed change:** Keep the accurately described 08/07 proxy date, but distinguish it from the supplied file’s 08/08 date. For example: “DEFM14A; supplied file dated 08/08/2013; proxy statement dated 08/07/2013.” The body does not independently establish an SEC submission timestamp, so I do **not** claim that 08/07 is a falsely quoted date or that the filename independently verifies the filing timestamp.

## C. Missing events

These are omissions of required event records, not additional faults for the same prices or dates discussed above. Each numbered item is one finding; C1 concerns four missing contact records.

### C1. First contacts with WDC, E, F and G are absent — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** #14 subtracts eight named parties from the 18-discussion population, but WDC/E/F/G have no separate first-contact events. Their NDA records (#16, #18, #20, #21) are their first individual rows. Earlier initiating-interest rows account for A/C/D, and #27 accounts for H, under the workbook’s Q4 recommendation.
- **Filing:** “On April 1, 2013, as directed by the special committee, BofA Merrill Lynch began contacting potential acquirers of sTec.” “In total, BofA Merrill Lynch had discussions with 18 prospective acquirers” (p. 27). The named NDA and presentation passages identify WDC/E/F/G among the participating prospects (pp. 27–28).
- **Rule:** C9 makes first contacts and executed NDAs separate events. C3 requires the aggregate step to reconcile after removing individually represented members; an NDA does not substitute for a Contact.
- **Proposed change:** Add the missing first-contact records, individually or as an appropriately bounded cohort, with reported or explicitly inferred timing rather than exact NDA-day contacts. Explain any inference from the aggregate discussions and subsequent participation. Keep total unique discussions at **18**, not 22, and retain Q4 for uncertain cohort membership. The deficiency is that four parties have been subtracted from the contact residual without their contact steps being represented; Q4’s defensible choice of membership is not itself an error.

### C2. H’s catch-up management presentation is only in a Note — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** #32 mentions the 05/10 presentation in its NDA Note; there is no access event for it. #33/#37 separately record WDC/D data-room access.
- **Filing:** “On May 10, 2013, sTec held a management presentation with Company H.” (p. 29). Earlier presentations to WDC/D/G/E occurred during the weeks of 04/08–04/22 (p. 28).
- **Rule:** C2 expressly includes “catch-up access for a late entrant” among information-access decisions earning a row.
- **Proposed change:** Add `Other material event`, H, Strategic, Count 1, Process 1 / Round 1, exactly 05/10, for its catch-up management presentation. Describe the earlier access of the established bidders without inventing a denial of data-room access to H. This also supplies the access event relevant to correcting #38’s conditions.

### C3. WDC receives different projections after price agreement — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** No row records the 06/19 disclosure; the ledger moves from Paul Hastings on 06/17 to the standstill-waiver dispute on 06/20.
- **Filing:** “The Operating Projections were available to all bidders that received access to the data room” (p. 45). “The Cost-Reduction Projections were also provided to WDC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, WDC’s financial advisor, on June 19, 2013, after the parties had agreed upon the per share merger consideration.” (p. 46).
- **Rule:** C2 requires a row for “projections issued, revised or withheld after bidding has begun,” where the filing reports the difference. Part A requires consulting the rest of the filing, not only the background.
- **Proposed change:** Add an `Other material event` dated 06/19, Process 1 / Round 2, recording that WDC and its adviser received the cost-reduction forecasts after agreement on price. Contrast those forecasts with the operating projections supplied to data-room bidders. The earlier common operating-projection access can be explained in the existing access Notes; do not invent its exact delivery date or a new price bid.

### C4. WDC’s named financial adviser is missing — CORE

- **Location / workbook:** No `Adviser` row for Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, on any date. WDC’s legal adviser is recorded, but its financial adviser is absent.
- **Filing:** “Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, WDC’s financial advisor, on June 19, 2013” (p. 46), in the passage quoted in C3. The merger-agreement summary also identifies Wells Fargo as the buyer’s financial adviser (p. 64).
- **Rule:** C5 requires other parties’ advisers when named, one relationship at the earliest date shown selected or acting, stating “first seen acting” when appropriate.
- **Proposed change:** Add `Adviser`, Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, WDC’s financial adviser, first seen acting 06/19/2013, Process 1 / Round 2. Leave bidder Type and Count blank. Do not call 06/19 its proven retention date or put it among the target’s advisers.
