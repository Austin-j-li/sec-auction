## 1. Findings

### FIND-1 (A, High, high confidence) — the residual non-submission cohort: the instruction discourages it, Alex records it in every large-cohort deal, and no model produced it

- **Instruction §10.2:** "**No exit row is required for every NDA signer.** Summary records the last observed activity and 'later participation not disclosed' when appropriate." §13 check 2: "There is **no universal NDA-minus-exits-minus-one-winner balance requirement**." §12.A: "If later only some firms are discussed, do not manufacture withdrawal dates for the others."
- **Alex:** Providence 6030 "16 parties | | 06/01/2016 | Drop"; Mac-Gray 6942 "16 financial bidders | 07/25/2013 | Drop"; Zep 6392 "19 parties | 04/14/2014 | Drop". Rationale, Providence note 5: "participation_count tries to count the number of bidders at different stages of the process … it should be **recoverable** from the number of contacts and the number of NDAs and the number of informal and formal bids that have previously been recorded in the database."
- **Consequence in the 12 workbooks.** Providence: not one of the four records any outcome for the 16 signers who did not submit an IOI; DeepSeek writes in its own check 2 that "no NDA-minus-exits-minus-one balance was applied" and in a question that "adding exits for the two non-submitters or the four financial non-signers would fabricate decisions the filing does not disclose". Mac-Gray: all four record the 16-signer residual **NDA** cohort (Count 16) and then Opus's Summary says "Their last observed activity is signing a confidentiality agreement; no offer, exclusion or [withdrawal is recorded]". So the stock of active bidders falls from 20 to 4 with no event; the fact lives in a Summary sentence, not in a stackable row.
- **Why it matters:** the number of active bidders per stage is the object Alex's estimation consumes; a Summary sentence does not survive stacking 44 deal ledgers.
- **Smallest fix:** keep §10.2's evidentiary bar, but flip the default *where the bar is met*: "Where a complete, compatible population supports inferred non-submission, record it as a **Did not submit** cohort row (Count = the residual, Decided by Unknown, Exit reason Not stated), not only as a Summary observation limit." Add to §11.4 block 5 a required per-stage reconciliation line: signers → submitters → advancing → unaccounted.
- **The fix needs a dating rule, because §10.2 currently forbids one** ("Do not infer their decision dates, motives, identities or voluntary withdrawal from that arithmetic"). Alex dates each residual at the moment it became observable, not at the moment it happened: Providence's 16 at the 06/01 committee meeting rather than the 05/19 IOI deadline; Mac-Gray's 16 at the 07/25 Special Committee rather than the 07/23 deadline; PetSmart's 8 at the 10/30 deadline itself. Proposed clause: "Working date = the deadline or the next target decision that reveals the population; Date basis = Inferred day or Relative only; the row asserts observation, not a decision date." Consistent with §8.1 and with his own priority that "the order of events must be precise" (summary H).

### FIND-2 (A/F, High, high confidence) — Pro scored as Major the exact arithmetic §10.2 authorises, and which Alex explicitly asks for

- **Instruction §10.2:** "A complete, compatible population can support inferred non-submission. For example, **if fifteen eligible participants and an exhaustive list of six submitters refer to the same solicitation and period, nine non-submitters may be recorded as an inferred cohort.**" (The example is PetSmart's own numbers.)
- **Pro, PS-GLM-04, Major:** "Fifteen NDA parties minus six IOI submitters is converted into exactly nine non-submitters … **Remove the exact residual cohort** because the unit mapping is not established." PS-OPUS-05, Major: same, "despite unresolved organizations/group correspondence".
- **Alex, PetSmart note 5:** "15 NDAs have been signed. Six IOIs have been made. 4 of them have been accepted into the final round … For the remaining 9 bidders with NDAs but without IOIs, they haven't been approached by the IB. They haven't stated the reason for dropping out, so that should be **recorded as unknown**." His hand data writes 8 of those 9 as individual Drop rows.
- **On the merits.** Filing: "the Company entered into confidentiality and standstill agreements with **15 potentially interested financial buyers, all 15 of which**, during the month of October engaged in due diligence" (p.23); "On October 30, **six of the potentially interested parties** submitted indications of interest. From October 30 to November 2 … J.P. Morgan spoke by telephone with **all of the potentially interested parties, including those that did not submit an indication of interest**" (p.24); and Reasons p.27 repeats "the 15 potentially interested parties that engaged in due diligence". Numerator and denominator are the **same noun and the same fifteen**, the window is one solicitation, and the filing positively asserts that non-submitters exist. The Reasons section's "first round indications of interest from **5 bidder groups**" bears on how the **six** submissions map into groups; it cannot change how many of the fifteen did not submit. **The unit-mismatch objection is over-refinement here.**
- GLM's row is fully compliant with §10.2 *and* §10.3 — "Not permanent withdrawals; agency and motives not established", Decided by = Unknown, flagged to question Q6 — and was still scored Major. Opus's row is compliant except for Decided by = Bidder, which §10.3 does forbid ("Inferred non-submission does not establish Bidder agency"); that alone is a Minor field fix, not a Major.
- **Smallest fix:** restate §10.2's example as an operative test (same population noun; identical solicitation and window; submitter list exhaustive; no individual identity, date or motive asserted) and add one sentence: "A residual cohort meeting this test is correct extraction, not a defect; residual unit-mapping doubts belong in Questions." Re-adjudicate PS-OPUS-05 → Minor (agency field only) and PS-GLM-04 → not a defect.

### FIND-3 (B/A, High, high confidence) — exclusivity: Alex closes the rivals out, the instruction pauses them and never reopens them

- **Instruction §10.1:** "Rival preference with continued participation is Target decision, not an exit. **Exclusivity does not establish permanent exits for all rivals:** record evidenced effects, including temporary Participation paused." §10.1 "Not selected at signing" is gated on "A bidder **demonstrably remained a live alternative** when the target signed with someone else."
- **Alex, collection instructions §3.6:** "Record '**DropTarget**' if target does not invite the bidder to the final round." Mac-Gray note 8: "on September 24, it becomes clear that out of the three remaining bidders, one was granted exclusivity, and **the other two are dropped out by the target**. So, the actual date of the dropout event is September 24, and there is certainty that there are exactly two dropped bidders." Penford note 8: "in some clearly stated cases, a target can decide to continue exclusive negotiations with the highest bidder, and so in these cases, **the target drops the remaining bidders**." Hand rows 6958/6959: Party A, Party B, DropTarget, 09/24/2013.
- **What the four models did on Mac-Gray:** Opus and DeepSeek → "Participation paused" 09/24, Decided by Target, **and no later row**, so Party A and Party B are still "paused" at the 10/14 signing; GLM → "Dropped by target" 09/21 (Pro, MG-GLM-05, **Major**: "Party A and Party B are coded Dropped by target on September 21 as if dated exclusion decisions were reported"); Sol → **no participation-outcome row for Party A or Party B at all**, which Pro did not flag anywhere in its six Mac-Gray findings.
- Net effect: the only workbook that closed the two rivals was penalised; two left them permanently open; one lost them entirely. Alex's own complaint in that note was the *date and fabrication* (a Sep 18 invented dropout), not the inference itself — his objection was "the actual date … is September 24", i.e. he endorses the inference and disputes the date. Pro's finding therefore gets the date right and the principle wrong.
- **Smallest fix (two parts):** (i) §10.1 — keep Participation paused for the exclusivity event, but require that **every participant left open at signing receives a closing row** (extend "Not selected at signing" by dropping the "demonstrably live" gate, or add a value such as *Displaced at signing*, Decided by Target, marked inferred); (ii) question for Alex: when exclusivity is negotiated 09/21–09/23 and executed 09/24, which date is the rivals' exit?

### FIND-6 (E with a gap, Medium, high confidence) — Count design matches Alex; what it cannot deliver is the *stock*

- **Alex, Providence note 5:** "participation_count is a constructed variable, and **it is not a fact that we extract** from the deal background." Mac-Gray note 2: "for two named bidders, their NDAs have been recorded separately. This only leaves 2 strategics and 16 financials that are unnamed and signed NDAs. **So the math has to check out.**"
- **Instruction §7.2/§7.3 match this closely:** "Count is a tally contribution, not a population balance"; "**No extra participation-count event is needed** when underlying contact, NDA or bid activity already records the fact. Nevertheless, a disclosed aggregate activity needs a ledger representation." §5.2's disjoint-residual rule and §12.A are Mac-Gray note 4 almost verbatim, and **all three parsable Mac-Gray workbooks got 20 = 1+1+1+1+16 exactly right** (Opus R021, GLM R029, DeepSeek R026), which is the specific arithmetic Alex complained about.
- Opus's Providence Summary block 5 is the model of what Alex asked for: per-stage SUMIFS with units named, plus "Letter-of-intent population reconciliation | Seven buyers advanced from round 1 (nine indications less two low bidders excluded) plus Party C as a late entrant gives eight eligible … Six letters were received and the filing states one strategic and one financial buyer elected not to submit: **6 + 2 = 8**."

With FIND-1 and FIND-4, outflows are recorded only where the filing states them, so the active-bidder stock is not derivable from the rows alone.
- **Pro complaints Alex would share (true positives):** MG-DS-01 — Party A counted in its own contact row **and** inside the 15-strategic cohort, giving 51 contact units (this is literally Providence note 5 and Mac-Gray note 2); PW-OPUS-02 — the same Party A overlap in Providence. **Over-refinement:** PW-GLM-04, "Two additional Class I railroads contribute exactly two extra contacts without resolving whether they belong to the eleven-strategic cohort" — the alternative (Opus's blank) yields a contact total of "29, plus 0–2 unresolved", i.e. exactly the "at least N" vagueness Alex objects to in PetSmart note 2 and Kraton note 1. This one is a genuine coin-flip; flag it to Alex rather than fixing it.

### FIND-9 (A, Medium, high confidence) — exits and reasons: the instruction's list covers Alex's five codes; two defaults diverge

Mapping: Drop → Withdrew / Did not submit + Not stated; DropBelowM → Value below market price; DropBelowInf → Value below earlier offer; DropAtInf → Value at earlier offer; DropTarget → Dropped by target. **Kraton note 4 is satisfied in full** ("Sometimes they drop out by claiming that their valuation is below the market value … Sometimes … at their indication of interest … sometimes … below their indication of interest"). Nothing of Alex's is missing; "Value at or below market price", "Would not improve earlier offer", "Lower offer than rivals" and "Terms or process" are additions he can collapse.

Three divergences:
1. **Agency when the loser says nothing and the target picks a winner.** Alex's default is a bidder-driven `Drop` (Penford 8, Medivation 6075). The instruction produces `Not selected at signing`, Decided by Target — or, after FIND-3, nothing at all.
2. **Silence with no stated reason.** Mac-Gray: "Party C did not submit a revised indication of interest … nor did it specify any reasons in connection therewith." Alex codes `Drop`, which in his vocabulary means the bidder informed the target it was leaving — a bidder-agency default the filing does not support here. Pro's MG-OPUS-03 and MG-GLM-04 (Decided by = Bidder without an affirmative decision statement) are therefore **true positives Alex would accept**, and §10.3's "Inferred non-submission does not establish Bidder agency" is a genuine improvement on his five-code scheme. Worth telling him that `Drop` will be split into `Withdrew` (communicated) and `Did not submit` (observed silence), both mapping back to his `Drop`.
3. **A stated refusal to improve is being lost in the structured field.** Filing: "Party B indicated that it would not increase its price." Alex: 6056 "Party B | Drop || Refused to increase offer". Opus, GLM and Sol all coded Party B `Not selected at signing`, Decided by Target, **Exit reason "Lower offer than rivals"**, parking the refusal in a separate `Bid reaffirmed` row; Pro did not flag it. §10.3 already says "Stated valuation, inability to maintain a price and refusal to improve are distinct … **Prioritize an informative stated constraint without changing actual agency**" — the rule exists and did not bind.
- **Smallest fix:** append an example to §10.3: "A bidder asked to improve who declines takes Exit reason **Would not improve earlier offer** even where Decided by is Target."

## 2. Answers to the six questions

**1. Does Alex need exits, even inferred ones, and where exactly is the instruction stricter?**
Yes.
