Overall judgment

The best workbooks are strong human-reviewable first passes, but none should enter structural estimation unchanged. The most consequential weaknesses are generally not missing headline prices. They are unsupported participant closures, overlapping cohort counts, overly strong condition assignments, and economically important facts that appear in prose but do not reach the structured fields.

A particularly important finding is that genuine quotations do not guarantee correct economic coding. All 1,024 ledger quotation anchors were source-locatable after normalization and review, yet several accompanying classifications remain unsupported or convention-dependent.
