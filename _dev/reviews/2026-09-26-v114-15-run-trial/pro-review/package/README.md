# Research and review materials

Austin Li and Alex Gorbenko study informal and formal bidding, information, selection and competition in takeover auctions. The extracted ledgers are intended as inputs to structural estimation, with human source review. They should reconstruct the actual sale process, including departures from a simple informal-then-formal auction and uncertainty that the filing cannot resolve.

This package contains:

- `filings/`: the complete supplied SEC filings for Mac-Gray, Providence & Worcester and sTec.
- `extractions/<deal>/A.xlsx` through `E.xlsx`: five anonymous extractions per filing, 15 workbooks total. Labels are randomized independently for each deal.
- `instruction_v1.14.md`: the exact instruction these extractions followed. Its opening section explains the research uses.
- `research_context/`: Alex's original collection instructions and voice-note document, supplied as research context. They predate the tested instruction and may contain earlier conventions or case interpretations.
- `MANIFEST.json`: an inventory and hashes of the files in this package.

Please read the actual files and use your own judgment to evaluate and score each extraction's fitness for this research. Choose a consistent scoring approach and explain your judgments with source evidence. Distinguish an extraction problem from any conflict or ambiguity in the supplied conventions.

Only author/document properties and file-creation metadata were normalized in the anonymous workbooks. All worksheet content, formulas, styles and other non-property package parts are byte-identical to the original outputs. The model-identity key, execution logs, previous evaluations and administrative material are absent.
