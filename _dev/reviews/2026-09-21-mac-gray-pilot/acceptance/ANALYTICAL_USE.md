# Mac-Gray analytical use restrictions

These restrictions apply even after final acceptance. Unknown source information is not an extraction error that can be repaired by choosing an exact value.

- **Population totals:** 20 eventual independent NDA bidder units (2 strategic, 18 financial) are supported. Of the 18 financial signers, B and C are individually dated; the other 16 are not. The reported 50 contacts are entities/parties and translate to 49–50 independent units depending on whether the original outreach count includes Pamplona separately. Contact is not entry.
- **Early live competition:** do not sum Counts down the ledger or treat the anonymous cohort's Sort date as its actual entry date. Round 1 has three named NDA signers by July 23, plus 0–16 anonymous signers with unknown timing. Party A was already a process participant through its June 21 bid, but was not admitted to the first confidential stage. Exact early live counts and individual bidder durations are unavailable. Analyses requiring point-valued early populations must handle the bounds/missingness explicitly or exclude those observations.
- **Round 2:** four named candidates were selected July 25; A's admission was conditional on its August 5 NDA. Four admitted candidates is not proof that every anonymous process participant had already left on July 25. Their participation outside the selected stage is not precisely dated.
- **Final stage:** the complete September 11 invitation set is four; three respond by September 18 and C does not. This stage can use the supported named population after the anonymous closure bound.
- **Exits:** the anonymous 16 closure by September 11 is a conservative reconciliation bound, not an observed collective exit on that day. Its Dropped by target label is an E14 inference, not evidence all16 were rejected for the same reason. A/B are inferred displaced by September 24 exclusivity. C gave no reason for not submitting. None of these facts supplies a bidder-revealed valuation ceiling or voluntary withdrawal motive.
- **Price basis:** B's $21.50 final package is B's own value of $19 cash plus performance-contingent options it valued at $2.50. Retain the strike, vesting, five-year horizon and exclusion of acquisitions. Do not treat $21.50 as guaranteed cash, an independently valued option package, or proof that B offered more certain value than CSC. The $594m financing commitment is not the acquisition's stated equity value.
- **Formality/conditions:** apply frozen E11 final-solicitation Formality separately from E12 Heavy exclusivity conditions. “Formal” does not mean unconditional or ready to sign. The winner's earlier conditional bids are not upgraded by eventual signing.
- **Time:** Sort date orders rows. Use When and Date from/to for evidence about actual timing. In particular, cohort, adviser, Moab nonrollover and exclusivity-request dates include intervals or upper bounds.
- **Commitment-term bids:** R01 remains pending. The current 58-event candidate has 13 Bid rows; this is not a frozen count of all economic-offer revisions until the fee/guarantee boundary is decided. Core price observations and the three-round map are unaffected by adding same-price commitment events.
- **Cutoff/scope:** evidence is the supplied December 4, 2013 proxy, including available annexes. No actual later closing or future special meeting is inferred. Referenced Annex D is absent from this HTML; voting-event dates are supported elsewhere in the supplied filing.

Readiness for a particular estimation design requires its treatment of these restrictions to be explicit. Ledger acceptance alone does not certify that a complete point-valued structural-estimation input can be constructed.
