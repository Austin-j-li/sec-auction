## Acceptance limitations

This was a lead review of a familiar development case, informed by the previous audit/adjudication. It is not a blind human benchmark or another fresh-reader experiment. No human active-review time was measured. The earlier bounded inventory remains a separate predeclared experiment; these later whole-source findings must not be backdated into its omission-recall denominator.

There is no inference of universal correctness from the checker pass. R01 remains a consequential research-convention decision; final acceptance and freezing are pending its disposition and any resulting verified correction.
