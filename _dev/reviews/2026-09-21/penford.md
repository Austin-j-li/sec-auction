## Confirmed source or instruction error

1. **Medium severity; high confidence: invented exclusivity of the revised projections.** Deal ledger **P42** (event #41, October 11) says the Adjusted Strategic Plan "went only to Deutsche Bank, 10/10." The source says, "The Adjusted Strategic Plan was provided to Deutsche Bank on October 10, 2014" (filing p. 43, P00789). It does **not** say other recipients were excluded. C2 says to identify who did not receive information only where the filing does so. This matters because the note turns an unobserved access difference into a claimed adviser-versus-bidder information asymmetry just before signing. Delete "only"; if the October 10 adviser delivery is to be separately rowed under C2's projection-delivery rule, give it its own October 10 Other material event and leave other recipients unknown. The current note already preserves the delivery fact, so that row choice is low-impact.

2. **Low severity; high confidence: August 21 projection version is overstated.** Deal ledger **P15** (event #14) identifies the projections shown to Ingredion on August 21 as "the Strategic Plan, last updated 08/28." The presentation included "financial projections" (p. 27, P00562), while the Strategic Plan was last updated **seven days later** (p. 43, P00789). The note can mean the plan family, but it should not imply the August 28 version was already delivered. Say "financial projections; the Strategic Plan was later updated August 28." This affects the information set at the August 21 meeting, not the bid price.
