## Findings

### 1. Party A is counted twice as a first strategic contact

**Confirmed instruction error; medium severity, high confidence.** `Deal ledger!M2` counts Party A's Q4 2015 approach as one first contact. `M7,C7,P7` (#6) then records **11** strategic March-wave contacts **including Party A**. `P00522` (p. 27) describes the original Party A meeting; `P00528` (p. 28) says the week-of-March-28 outreach contacted **"11 potential strategic buyers (including Party A)"**. Given the workbook's one continuous process (`Questions!A2:F2`), Party A's March follow-up is a repeat contact, and C9 says **"A repeat communication within the same process is not a new first contact."** V0012-V0013 warns specifically that Providence repeat strategic contacts inflated `participation_count`. As encoded, summing first-contact Count across `M2,M7,M8,M11` yields 32 (1+11+18+2) despite 31 distinct contacted parties on the stated/assumed membership: 11 in the strategic wave including previously met A, 18 financial, and two additional railroads. **Economic consequence:** outreach size and contact-to-NDA conversion are biased. **Smallest correction:** make #6 a residual cohort of **10 other strategic contacts** (`C7,M7,P7`) and retain Party A's first-contact row #1; explain that 10 plus A reconciles to the filing's 11-person wave. Reflect this in `Rounds!D2` and `Deal facts!B18` only if their wording implies 11 new strategic first contacts. The two **"additional Class I railroads"** (`P00530`) remain separate at `M11=2`; their membership is otherwise unknown.

### 2. Party B's August 4 condition level conflicts with Alex's later desired coding, but follows v1.11

**Newer Alex guidance mismatch, not a confirmed source error; medium severity, high confidence.** `Deal ledger!E45,H45:L45` (#44) records Party B's returned merger draft as a $24 Formal `Bid reaffirmed` with **Light** conditions. Alex `V0030` explicitly calls for an August 4 **"formal unconditional offer"**. The filing says Party B counsel returned a revised merger draft on August 4 (`P00555`, p. 31), but also that Party B's on-site diligence continued **through August 11** (`P00554`, p. 31). The original LOI was **"subject to an expedited diligence review"** (`P00540`, p. 29). Current C12 specifically says a returned draft does not show that diligence conditions disappeared, and C14 makes limited/expedited diligence Light, while None requires affirmative readiness. The old `deal_details!AC6054,AG6054` treats August 4 as an executed/confirmed bid after diligence, which the filing timing does not support. **Economic consequence:** a `None` flag would portray a fully unconditional finalist eight days early. **Smallest decision:** retain Light under v1.11 unless Alex explicitly changes the condition convention; keep the voice disagreement in the review record. The additional August 12 price confirmation at `Deal ledger!E49,H49:L49` is supported by `P00561` and C12.

## Limits
