## Findings

### 1. Sixteen financial exits are assigned too early and to a single exit mechanism

**Confirmed overstatement of evidence; medium severity, high confidence.** `Deal ledger!B25,E25,M25,T25,V25` (#24) closes all 16 residual financial NDA signers as `Dropped by target` by **July 25**, and `Rounds!E2,J2` treats all 20 signers as already in round 1 and the residual as out at that date. The underlying count of 16 is sound: the filing says 20 signers over two months, comprising Party A and CSC/Pamplona plus 18 financial bidders including B and C (`P00695`, p. 32); `20 - 2 strategic - 2 named financial = 16`. The date and mechanism are not: those agreements occurred **"Over the next two months"** after the June 24 decision (`P00695`), whereas the July 25 committee advanced only **"four interested bidders"** (`P00710-P00714`, pp. 33-34). An unknown number of the 16 may have signed after July 25. The first unequivocally complete later invitation is the August 27 letter to the four (`P00726`, p. 35).

Under v1.11 C3/C10/C16, an inferred exit requires a supported live period and transition; for an eligible non-submitter, `Did not submit` takes precedence over an inferred target exclusion. `Questions!A5:F5` (Q4) acknowledges the timing problem but recommends retaining the July 25 closure. This is not proof that all 16 were actively rejected on July 25. **Economic consequence:** a researcher could place 16 live bidders into, or remove them from, a round on an unsupported day and misread selection rates. **Smallest correction:** keep the exact 16-person NDA residual, remove the all-16 July 25 exit assertion, and represent the split between July 23 non-submitters and later signers as unknown. If one closure row is required, bound it by the complete August 27 four-party invitation and flag its mixed/uncertain route; update `Rounds!E2,J2` and Q4 accordingly. Alex's newer notes ask specifically for exact NDA arithmetic and a July 25 second stage (`V0041`, `V0044-V0045`), but do not establish that every signer existed before that date. The older `deal_details!N6934,N6942` also uses a 16-person financial residual and a July 25 drop; it cannot settle the timing.

The underlying rows correctly show the two directions: Mac-Gray instructed BofA to sound out Party A on April 8 (`Deal ledger!B3:E3`, `P00657-P00658`, p. 27), then Party A sent an **"unsolicited proposal"** for $17-$19 on June 21 before the organized auction (`Deal ledger!B9:K9`, `P00683`, p. 30). Alex calls this **"a bit of both"** and leaves target versus bidder initiation open (`V0038-V0039`); the old sheet likewise distinguishes `deal_details!AC6928` Target Interest from `AC6931` Bidder Sale. C6 asks for each initiating step and a Deal-facts assessment; it does not dictate one binary label. **Economic consequence:** a binary target-origin variable would erase the bidder-triggered June proposal. **Smallest correction:** make `Deal facts!B10` say the target made the first approach, while Party A independently triggered the priced proposal, and classify overall initiation as mixed/ambiguous if a single category is needed. No ledger row needs to be added.
