## Confirmed source or instruction errors

1. **Medium severity; high confidence: exact non-submitter count is unsupported.** Deal ledger event #40, **C41/M41/P41**, says exactly 13 other NDA signers did not submit by September 17; Rounds **E4/J4** reports 17 live units and 13 non-submitters as exact. The filing says 14 outreach parties signed NDAs, then reports IOIs from E, G and CLP (p. 32-33, P00418, P00420-P00430); it never identifies whether G was one of the 14, and E's membership is uncertain. The 13-person **NDA cohort** after subtracting known signer CLP (**M30=13**) is arithmetically valid, but **13 non-submitters** is not: if G or E was also in the cohort, it submitted. Existing Question **Q4/C5:F5** admits this uncertainty, yet the exit and Rounds turn one branch into an exact fact. C3 requires blank Count for an uncertain residual and a supported bound; C16's Did not submit subtraction must net out identified submitters. The smallest correction is to leave **M41** blank, identify the possible G/E overlap in **C41/P41**, state the conditional non-submitter range (11-13), and make **Rounds E4/J4** a bounded/uncertain live count. This changes the inferred attrition rate, not the conclusion that the process met the multi-NDA screen.

2. **Medium severity; high confidence: Company E's January 4 exit reason confuses scope with price.** Event #65, **N66/P66**, uses **Would not improve earlier offer**. The actual request was whether E would "re-engage on an acquisition of 100%"; E declined and said its **35% tender** proposal would end after January 4 (p. 36, P00458). There is no reported refusal to raise the $2.00 price. V0140 asks for precise bidder-versus-target drop reasons, and C16 reserves the chosen reason for a bidder asked to improve its offer that declines. Keep **E66=Withdrew** and the date, but use **Terms or process** (or another scope-specific stated reason) and say E declined to return to a whole-company bid. The current reason would misclassify a change in acquisition scope as valuation resistance.

## Unresolved convention or source ambiguity

1. **Medium severity; medium confidence: the 2018-to-2019 break is underdetermined.** Company A withdrew June 20, 2018 (P00402), but Liontree's approximately 12 outreach contacts have no end date and its mandate ran to December 31. Company C's NDA begins only in "March 2019" (P00404), so a contact as late as December could leave less than C7's approximately three-month break. The Company B combination from October 2018 to March 2019 is excluded as Synacor's own merger-of-equals approach under C1 (P00403), but if treated as a sale it also bridges the interval. Process restart #9 **E10:O10**, Deal facts **B11**, and Question **Q1/D2:F2** acknowledge both routes. Keep three processes as a reasonable reading, but do not treat the first boundary as source-established without a convention on how to use undated outreach.
2. **Medium severity; medium confidence: September 17 deadline was not demonstrably hard.** Rounds **G4=Enforced**, Deadline event #39 **P40**, and Question **Q5/C6:F6** acknowledge the alternative. E submitted a revised $2.00 LOI on September 18 after a September 17 best-price request (P00429-P00431); H was invited to bid September 22 (P00433-P00436). Under C11, a late bid requires proof the bidder was given that due date. The filing does not say whether E, which first approached before the 37-party outreach, received it, while H entered after it. Enforced remains defensible because the next selection used the bids then in hand and no same-round extension is reported. Do not label E a proven late bidder; retain human review of **G4**.
3. **Medium severity; medium confidence: December 29 reference to CLP's "offer" may indicate a missing late-December bid.** The filing compares I's portal price to "CLP's offer" on December 29 (p. 35, P00452), yet CLP is only said to be considering re-engagement on December 18-21 (P00450) and its next dated proposal is January 4 (P00457). The comparison might refer to CLP's standing September IOI, or be retrospective prose; it does not establish an amount or a new submission. Event #58 **P59** and event #63 **B64/P64** should not acquire an invented bid, but add a Question or note explaining why no intervening priced row was coded. If another filing passage establishes a new offer, C12 would require a distinct Bid in round 3.
4. **Low severity; medium confidence: final-stage opening date.** The committee chose CLP and decided to "commence diligence and negotiation of a definitive agreement" on January 6 (P00460), while the LOI was executed January 7 (P00461). Round-open event #69 **B70/G70**, Rounds **C8/D8**, use January 7. Under C8, the decision could open round 5 on January 6, as Penford's analogous board decision does. The prior $2.00 revision is only bounded **by** January 6 and logically precedes the decision, so its round-4 coding survives either way. Existing process Question **Q1** flags the map, but should mention this one-day boundary choice if adjudicated.
5. **Low severity; medium confidence: Company I's exit and partial bidders in the screen.** Event #64 **E65/O65** infers I withdrew by January 4 from Canaccord's report that only E and H remained as other interested parties (P00457). That report may refer only to whole-company interest; Question **Q8/C9:F9** correctly leaves the alternative live-to-signing closure open. Deal facts **B13** counts portal bidders' confidentiality agreements in the process-2/process-3 auction screen; C1 does not explicitly say whether a prospective acquirer of only a segment qualifies for a whole-company auction. This screen convention should be decided without changing the correctly labeled Other-scope bids.
