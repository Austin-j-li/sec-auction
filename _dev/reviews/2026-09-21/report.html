Verdict

The eight v1.11 workbooks are useful research drafts, but the batch is not ready for unattended estimation. Most disclosed bid prices and major transaction events are captured well. Remaining errors concentrate in the variables that matter most for this project: which bidders are live, when they leave, what constitutes a round, what business is being acquired, and what the bidder knew or still needed to do.

PetSmart and Penford are the strongest cases and need narrow corrections.
