## Consequential rule changes and remaining choices

1. **Contacts and counts.** The old written rules (PDF sec. 3.4, pp. 6-7) emphasize signed NDAs; V0011-V0014, V0040-V0045 and Alex's own V0172 demand separate first contacts and NDA events, deduped against re-contacts and memoranda. V0013 calls `participation_count` constructed rather than an extracted fact. v1.11 C3/C9 already takes this approach: one initial-contact row where supported, NDA signed only for qualifying execution, residual cohort arithmetic, no `participation_count` event. Voice V0056's claim that a specific PetSmart "at least 27" was exact is case-specific and must be established from filing, not generalized to every lower bound; v1.11 C3 correctly preserves qualifiers unless exact arithmetic supports a total.
2. **Rounds, timing and deadlines.** The March written rule (sec. 3.7, p. 8) centers a final round; newer V0025-V0028, V0042/V0044, V0072, V0082, V0108-V0110 and V0122-V0124 recognize multiple informal stages, unannounced final stages, distinct processes, and unenforced deadlines. Alex's own V0168-V0169 asks for human review at boundary evidence. v1.11 C7/C8/C10/C11 and Rounds cover these distinctions, including due-date outcome and revisions. The exact C7 three-condition break test, C8's "smallest round map" preference and C11's outcome precedence are operational conventions beyond a universally settled voice rule; retain Questions when a filing supports a plausible alternative.
3. **Formality and conditionality.** The older written sec. 3.5 (p. 7) classifies *any* range as informal and tends to equate final-round solicitation with formal status. The newer voice expressly keeps Mac Gray's best-and-final range formal (V0048), treats sTec's late marked-up IOI as formal despite its name (V0125), and separates formal label from none/light/heavy conditions (difficult/red V0019-V0020). v1.11 C13-C14 follows that distinction and does not turn an exclusivity request alone into informal status. Alex also wants conditionality recoverable for later estimation (V0046-V0049, V0174). v1.11's four-level field plus free-text Note captures the bid's reported condition, but **whether exclusivity and financing guarantee status require separate structured fields** for downstream reinterpretation remains an Alex/Austin schema decision, not a case error.
4. **Reconfirmations and exits.** V0030-V0031 requests a new formal offer row when a standing bid is reaffirmed with revised markup; V0074 suggests Penford's final $19 confirmation; V0104 calls for recording a return to a previous price. v1.11 C12 now has bounded `Bid reaffirmed` and price-reversion rules, but the exact evidentiary threshold for "contacted to confirm" versus actual bidder confirmation should be surfaced in the case Question if the filing is equivocal. V0060, V0070, V0083 and Alex's own V0173 ask for precise, noninvented exit agency/reasons; v1.11 C16 allows explicitly marked inferred closures. **Whether every inferred closure should be an event rather than a still-live/unknown status** remains a research convention for Alex if it materially changes risk sets.
5. **Scope and comparability.** The March written sec. 3.5 (p. 7) says to omit partial-company bids. Newer V0097-V0102 and Alex's V0179-V0180 demand that such deals and units be flagged; Meredith may remain descriptive despite an estimation exclusion. v1.11 C1/C15 records `Other-scope bid`, flags lack of whole-company bids, and leaves unsupported per-share conversion blank. It does not collect a structured enterprise-value, net-debt or share-count normalization panel. **Whether to build such a panel, and whether Meredith enters an estimation sample, are downstream design decisions.** V0117's proposed target-stock-price path for sTec likewise needs a separate market-data convention; it cannot be supplied from filing evidence alone under current rules.
6. **Human review workflow.** Alex's V0164-V0175 calls for interactive review of round/process boundaries, unknown types, NDA populations, uncertain exits and conditions, with event order more important than exact day. v1.11 Part D uses post-extraction Questions and flags, not an interactive stop. Whether to add real-time review is a workflow/product decision, not evidence that an individual ledger row is wrong. The case reviews should report the actual v1.11 Question quality.

Other clear alignments: v1.11 C6 preserves Mac Gray's mixed target/bidder initiation sequence (V0038-V0039); C4 excludes PetSmart Longview's rollover from bidding-group formation (V0064); C5 reconciles adviser name changes (V0029); C15 treats cash earnouts as cash with contingent payment (V0084); and B2 separately records agreement execution and public announcement (V0032, V0085, V0103, V0111, V0126). These are tests of implementation in each case, not unresolved changes to the instruction.
