## Review requirements

Read the complete relevant filing background section, then investigate other filing sections for acquirer type, advisers, projections/information access, consideration and agreement dates. Read every ledger row, every Rounds entry, every Question, and all Deal facts. Reconstruct process and round boundaries and bidder participation independently. Reconcile exact versus qualified contacts, NDA signers, advancing sets, bidding groups, and exits. Audit every price-bearing bid for amount, date bounds/order, formality, conditionality, consideration, recipient and round. Look for omitted material events as well as incorrect rows. Do not mistake quote occurrence for semantic support.

Classify findings separately:

1. Confirmed source or instruction error: the filing or current v1.11 rule establishes the correction.
2. Newer Alex guidance mismatch: the voice guidance differs from extraction or an older reference; identify whether the current instruction itself causes the mismatch.
3. Unresolved convention or source ambiguity: multiple defensible codings; no forced correction.
4. Harmless representation difference or stylistic issue: do not inflate quality failures.

Do not treat older hand-coded entries as ground truth. Do not upgrade an offer retrospectively because it later wins. Distinguish formal offer evidence from heavy/light/no conditions, round participation from mere live participation, and missing bidder identity from known population totals. An existing Question appropriately exposing ambiguity is a strength, but does not excuse contradicting a known fact.

For each finding give severity (high/medium/low), confidence, sheet and exact cells/event IDs, short source quotation plus filing paragraph/page, relevant voice paragraph or reference cell, rule section where applicable, economic consequence, and smallest correction or decision needed. Include rejected apparent errors when they explain important differences from Alex.

## Deliverable

Write an evidence-dense Markdown report for your assigned cases under _dev/reviews/2026-09-21/, one file per case. Include a clear verdict (strong with minor corrections / usable after targeted corrections / material rework / convention-dependent), source coverage, Alex hand-coding coverage, findings, what is correctly captured, unresolved decisions and limits. No invented percentage accuracy or precision/recall: there is no adjudicated complete gold standard. Use exact evidence, not repeated general QA prose.

Only write your assigned report files. You are not alone in this workspace; preserve others' edits. Return a short summary to the parent with the most material findings and report paths. Use narrow searches, structured parsing, and full reading of bounded relevant sections instead of loading full filings into context. The root will challenge material findings and synthesize the final verdict.
