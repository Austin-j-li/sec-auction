## Boundaries and deliberate exclusions

- Routine board consideration, fiduciary presentations, the September 1 sequence of legal drafts, and termination-fee/governing-law bargaining do not each create rows under E2. The employment closing condition and material financing changes above directly change an offer's commitment and therefore require scrutiny under E10.
- “Expected to execute within 30 days of exclusivity” is not automatically a stated 30-day diligence condition. Conditions must follow the actual bid evidence; this inventory does not pre-adjudicate B's classification.
- No additional round opens merely because the target requests improved final offers, receives them, selects Insight, or executes exclusivity after the final round has opened.
- S12 is a participation constraint plus an eventual reported outcome. The text does not give an exact day on which the two bidders declined; do not convert a temporal reading into an asserted exact fact.
- The inventory is finite and bounded. It supplies no completeness denominator for the rest of the filing and no claim of human review time saved.
