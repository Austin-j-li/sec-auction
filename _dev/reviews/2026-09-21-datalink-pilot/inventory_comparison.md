## Additional lead observations before the fresh review

- #24 and #28 set exact counts of 5 and 8 while Q5 explicitly admits that the unnamed submitters' NDA membership is not established. Part B and E3 require qualified counts in that circumstance. Merely setting Inferred = Y does not authorize exactness. Q5 itself supplies candidate ranges 5–7 and 8–9, which need arithmetic verification.
- #48 places Insight's new pre-closing accounting/auditor requirements in Other material event, leaves the bid fields empty, and dates the event September 28 while acknowledging a September 26 draft in the Note. E10 requires a dated offer change when a communicated material condition changes. Determine the earliest supported communication of each distinct condition before proposing a correction.
- #63 and #65 justify Light using diligence completed on November 2–6. That later completion cannot establish the offer's condition level on November 1 or at the November 2 call. Check what the filing actually supports as of each offer.
- The raw workbook and the inventory remain unchanged. These are lead candidates awaiting the source-backed adjudication packet, not accepted research data or findings supplied to the fresh reviewer.
