## Source and interpretation conventions

Printed filing page numbers, including Annex A page A-22; unnumbered cover explicitly labelled. Quotations are contiguous exact text after HTML entity decoding and whitespace normalization; no spliced passages. All evidence excerpts are at most 30 words.

Inventory IDs are finite evidence units, not prescribed workbook row numbers or a strictly sorted ledger. Some units may be folded into notes under C2; required separate bids, NDA/contact events, re-entries, exits, openings and signing/announcement cannot be silently merged away. An inventory unit may require multiple type-specific or bidder-specific ledger rows. Sources establish facts; convention_or_inference fields establish researcher coding, not additional reported facts.

Facts and convention-based inferences are distinguished below. “Formal,” “Heavy,” round boundaries, residual exits and sort keys are researcher classifications, not necessarily words used by the filing. The 49 inventory units are not a demand for exactly 49 workbook rows.

- **target:** Providence and Worcester Railroad Company (PWRR)
- **acquirer:** Genesee & Wyoming Inc. (G&W); Pullman Acquisition Sub Inc. is its acquisition shell, one bidder
- **acquirer type:** Strategic
- **agreed price and consideration:** USD 25.00 cash per common share; preferred shares deemed converted 100:1 with accrued/unpaid dividends before common-share treatment, subject to preferred dissenters exception
- **signed:** 2016-08-12
- **announced:** 2016-08-15 before market open
- **filing type and date:** DEFM14A; mounted filename identifies 2016-09-20 filing. Proxy itself dated 2016-09-19; do not represent filename date as a quoted printed filing-date disclosure.
- **background pages:** 27–32 inclusive
- **initiation:** Bidder-led exploratory Party A trigger followed by target-led auction; mixed is accepted
- **number of processes:** 1
- **auction screen:** Met: 25 initial independent buyer NDAs; Party C later NDA implies 26 cumulative against p.33 summary total of 25. Flag discrepancy.
- **whole company bids:** Yes; Q4 Party A equity/JV exploration is separately distinguished, no whole-company offer established then
- **currency and units:** USD per common share assuming preferred conversion; total-equity figures are supplementary, not bases for invented per-share conversions
- **target financial advisers:** GHF, business acquired by BMO August 1, 2016; one continuing relationship
- **target legal advisers:** Hinckley, Allen & Snyder LLP; unnamed STB counsel consulted, no invented identity
- **buyer legal adviser:** Simpson Thacher & Bartlett LLP for G&W

Party A’s exploratory interest in equity or a joint venture prompted PWRR to engage GHF and launch a broader sale process. The initial outreach produced 25 NDAs and nine IOIs, after which seven bidders advanced to diligence. New entrant Party C brought the next stage to eight participants, six of whom submitted LOIs. PWRR selected Party B and G&W; D and E briefly returned, but neither remained through the final contest, and F provided financing support rather than a new independent bid. PWRR initially prioritized Party B at $24, but G&W raised its cash offer to $25 on August 12 and Party B declined to increase. The agreement was signed August 12 and announced August 15.

Evidence: “G&W, a Delaware corporation, owns or leases 121 freight railroads worldwide” (p. 21). “Merger Sub, a Rhode Island corporation, is a wholly-owned subsidiary of G&W” (p. 21). “will be canceled and will be converted automatically into the right to receive $25.00 in cash, without interest.” (p. 27). “the Company and G&W executed the merger agreement” (p. 32). “The Company issued a press release publicly announcing the transaction on Monday, August 15, 2016” (p. 32). “This proxy statement is dated September 19, 2016” (p. unnumbered cover/letter). “Each of the potential strategic buyers and 14 potential financial buyers subsequently executed confidentiality agreements.” (p. 28). “After executing a confidentiality agreement, Party C was provided the memorandum concerning the Company provided to the other potential buyers.” (p. 29). “25 of whom entered into confidentiality agreements with the Company and received information related to the Company and six of whom submitted non-binding letters of intent” (p. 33). “the Board approved the subcommittee’s recommendation and the Company engaged GHF” (p. 27). “BMO (which acquired the business of GHF on August 1, 2016)” (p. 30). “the Company’s legal counsel, Hinckley, Allen & Snyder LLP” (p. 28). “Simpson Thacher & Bartlett LLP (which we refer to as “Simpson Thacher”), G&W’s legal counsel” (p. 31). “the Merger Agreement, dated as of August 12, 2016” (p. 1).
