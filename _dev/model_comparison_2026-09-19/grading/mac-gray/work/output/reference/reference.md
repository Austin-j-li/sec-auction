## Deal and scope

Mac-Gray explored alternatives and approached Party A before launching organized outreach in June 2013. The banker contacted 50 prospects and 20 signed confidentiality agreements over the ensuing two months. Four bidders advanced to staged diligence, with strategic bidders receiving more restricted information than financial bidders. September 11 opened a final solicitation; Party C did not respond, while Party B offered cash plus contingent equity options and CSC offered committed all-cash funding. CSC increased to $21.25, obtained exclusivity September 24 and completed negotiations after an extension. The agreement was signed October 14 and publicly announced October 15.

Target: Mac-Gray Corporation. Buyer: CSC ServiceWorks through Spin Holdco/CSC Fenway; source label CSC/Pamplona, one Strategic bidder. Agreed price: $21.25 cash per share. Signed October 14, announced October 15, 2013. Filing: DEFM14A dated December 4, 2013 (unnumbered cover). Auction screen: Met, 20 cumulative NDA signers, 2 Strategic and 18 Financial. Target advisers: BofA Merrill Lynch and Goodwin Procter. Buyer counsel: Kirkland & Ellis. Agreement §5.11 identifies Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank Securities and Evercore as buyer-group fee-entitled bankers/brokers; precise mandates and retention dates unstated.

One target sale process. Initiation is target-led with unsolicited Party A proposal and Moab governance context. Mixed/activist-influenced is acceptable if explained without inventing an express sale demand. Earlier Mac-Gray consideration of buying CSC is outside this target-sale process.
