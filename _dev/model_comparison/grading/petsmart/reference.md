## Deal and scope

PetSmart agreed on December 14, 2014 to acquisition by Argos Holdings Inc./Argos Merger Sub Inc. for $83 per share in cash. The Buyer Group is Financial; Longview provides shareholder rollover support. Signing and announcement are separate December 14 events. The definitive proxy is dated February 2, 2015; background is printed pp. 21–26. This is one activist-influenced/mixed continuing sale process, with two rounds and an auction screen met by 15 financial buyer NDAs. Deal-fact evidence is in the inventory and JSON.
