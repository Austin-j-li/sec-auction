## Verdict

The proposal has the right shape: one coded column per condition the researchers named, a marker for antitrust, and Not stated kept apart from negatives. Test-coding bids in nine of the twelve filings shows the value sets are not ready. Financing has no value for the commonest firm statement, "not subject to any financing condition". Due diligence has no value for the commonest weak one, "subject to due diligence". Regulatory folds risk allocation into a value that asserts concern, and Antitrust fires on FCC and STB divestitures. The no-carry-forward rule is sound in itself but conflicts with how E10 and E12 judge the summary level, so the checker will report contradictions the expert cannot resolve. Open decision 1 rests on a wrong premise: E13 already puts package values, CVR included, into the price cells. With the value fixes and cross-reference edits below, the design will work.

## Issues, ranked

**1. Financing cannot code "no financing condition".** Pfizer's August 8 proposal "stated that it was not subject to any financing condition", medivation line 941, and the background never says how Pfizer would pay. Not needed requires cash on hand and Committed requires signed letters, so the firmest financing statement in the corpus codes Not stated, there and on the $77.00 and $81.50 bids after it. Kraton's Parent "agreed that financing would not be a condition" though it "required debt financing", line 1582, and Synacor's CLP was "not contingent upon third party debt financing", line 1763. Two collisions: Mac-Gray Party A was "to be financed by outstanding credit facilities or affiliated equity sources" yet "did not include a firm financing commitment", lines 1655 and 1657, fitting Not needed and Contingent; Pamplona "was committed to provide 100% of the capital", line 1650, with no letter until signing, fitting neither. Replacement: "`Not needed`: cash on hand, existing facilities, all stock, or a sponsor or parent reported as committing the full price. `Committed`: signed commitment letters reported. `No condition`: the bid states no financing condition and the source is not reported. `Contingent`: a financing condition, a highly confident letter, financing reported as not yet arranged or still being explored, or any part uncommitted. Contingent wins where the filing reports both a source and the absence of a commitment."

**2. Regulatory mixes concern, allocation and mere mention; Antitrust over-fires.** No concern triggers on mention alone, so Kraton's Parent, whose markup "deleted the hell or high water regulatory provisions", line 1578, codes No concern. Risk borne by the bidder exists only as a sub-case of Concern: Gray "committed to divest its Flint-Saginaw-Bay City station", meredith line 4072, while the board called Gray's profile "comparatively clean", line 4139. Citing "a regulatory reverse fee" as evidence collides with decision 3. "Divesting an overlapping business" marks Meredith's FCC duopoly divestiture and P&W's STB review, "G&W owned connecting railroads", line 1419, as antitrust. Replacement: "Regulatory: `No concern`, the filing reports the target, its advisers or the bidder expecting no material obstacle or a clean or prompt path; `Concern`, a reported risk, expected divestiture, second request, long timeline or closing doubt for this bid; `Not stated`; `Varies`. New marker **Reg. risk borne**: Y when the bid commits the bidder to absorb regulatory risk by a divestiture commitment or hell-or-high-water undertaking. Antitrust: Y only when the filing names an antitrust or competition law or authority. Delete 'Y requires Regulatory to be filled'." Open decision 2: bidder-borne risk should not force Heavy; for use 3 it raises commitment, like committed financing.

**3. Due diligence has no default and no precedence.** Unqualified conditions are the norm: "satisfactory completion of Pfizer's due diligence" with signing "in approximately one week", medivation line 940; "subject to customary diligence review", meredith line 3833; "subject to additional due diligence", kraton line 1605. Gray "confirmed it had completed substantive diligence", meredith line 4071, fits Complete and Confirmatory; the board then demanded "confirmation that all diligence was complete", line 4155. Mac-Gray's next stage was "more in-depth and confirmatory due diligence", line 1625, both words at once. Replacement: "`Substantive`: further diligence not described as confirmatory, limited or expedited; or a stated period of two weeks or more; or an exclusive period. `Confirmatory`: only confirmatory, limited or expedited diligence; a stated period under two weeks; or substantive diligence reported complete without a statement that none remains. `Complete`: the filing says no diligence remains. A stated condition beats `Not begun`. Where two values fit, take the heavier."

**4. E13 already answers open decision 1, the other way.** E13 says "A stated per-share package value goes in the price cells as the total offer value", and All cash is Yes "including an earnout or contingent value right paid in cash". G&W's "$21.15 per share" that "included $20.02 cash at closing and $1.13 in the form of a contingent value right", p-w line 1349, is $21.15 under E13 and $20.02 under the draft. The column also needs a value, not a maximum: $1.13 is a stated value; Party B "valued the Party B options at $2.50 of incremental value per share", mac-gray line 1696, and those options are contingent securities the draft would count as stock; Datalink Party A's "at least $10.30 ... as high as $10.75 per share if certain financial criteria were met", line 1497, is a range under E13 and an earnout under the draft. Replacement: amend E13 to "Price cells hold the upfront per-share value; a contingent payment goes in the CVR columns." Rename the column "CVR/earnout value: the per-share amount the filing states for the contingent payment; the Note says whether it is a maximum, a face amount or the bidder's valuation." Add: "A security whose payout depends on performance is CVR/earnout, not stock. A price that rises if criteria are met is upfront at the low figure with the increment as CVR."

**5. No-carry-forward contradicts how the summary is judged.** E10 carries Conditions "as they stand at that date" and E12 treats the level as a state. New Mountain's February 26 revision reports only price and exclusivity, zep line 1545, seven days after its highly confident letter; the columns say Not stated, the summary says Heavy, and checker rule 1 sees nothing. Pfizer's winning $81.50 bid, line 966, gets Not stated in every column. Replacement: "A bid row whose condition columns are all Not stated takes Conditions = Unclear, except: (a) Bid reaffirmed copies the standing column values, Note 'carried from #n'; (b) where the filing says the terms were otherwise unchanged, as in 'otherwise materially unchanged', meredith line 4114, or 'otherwise on the transaction terms previously proposed on May 28', stec line 1449, copy the prior row's values and write 'Terms: as #n (p. x)'."

**6. Stock % edge cases.** Bidder E's "cash and stock transaction (with stock representing 50% to 75% of the consideration)", pepco line 2246, fits no value: not one number, and Part stock is defined for bids with no figure. "$25.00 per share in cash", p-w line 1399, and "$80.70 per share, in cash", petsmart line 1346, do not literally say "all cash". Meredith holders receive $14.51 cash plus NMG SpinCo shares, and nothing says whether spun-off shares are consideration. Replacement: "Stock % is a text column; the checker accepts a number to one decimal, a range 'a-b' where the filing states one, or the three codes. 0 when the filing says the price is in cash or all cash. Shares of a spun-off company distributed beside a cash merger are not consideration; name them in the Note."

**7. Exclusivity boundary and timing.** Zep's board recorded that New Mountain "was not prepared to move forward ... without an exclusivity agreement", line 1550, though the letter only "requested" one; P&W's Party D "requested that the Company commit" and, refused, "would not proceed", lines 1376 to 1380. Party B's "within 30 days from the date it was granted exclusivity", datalink line 1571, assumes rather than asks. Ingredion asked about exclusivity in a call four days after its bid, penford line 1348, which is a D2 Exclusivity changed row, not a bid term. Replacement: "`Required`: the bid, or the bidder's stated willingness to continue, is conditioned on exclusivity, including a bidder that stops when refused. `Requested`: asked for, assumed, or a draft exclusivity agreement supplied, without a condition. A request made while the bid stands and before its next revision counts for that bid and also earns its D2 row."

**8. Checker rules: one wrong, several missing, two stale references.** Regulatory = Concern forcing Heavy makes every Pepco bid Heavy, because the concern there is process-wide, lines 2237 and 2250. There is no Light rule. Exclusivity is unmentioned; say Required does not enter the summary, matching E11. The summary cannot later be computed while a right to reprice or a bidder shareholder vote lives only in the Note. F.4 ("has All cash, Formality and Conditions") and D2 ("price cells, All cash, Formality and Conditions blank") still name the deleted column. Replacement: "Heavy required when Financing = Contingent, Due diligence = Substantive, or Regulatory = Concern with Reg. risk borne blank. Light requires Due diligence = Confirmatory, or Financing in {Not needed, Committed, No condition} with Due diligence in {Complete, Not stated}. None requires Due diligence = Complete, Financing in {Not needed, Committed, No condition}, Regulatory not Concern. CVR/earnout value filled requires CVR/earnout = Y."

**9. Scope and evidence.** Antitrust as an entry barrier never reaches a bid row: PetSmart kept Industry Participant out for "the very high risk that an acquisition by or a combination with Industry Participant would not receive antitrust clearances", line 1323, and it never bid. Eight coded cells with a page each will break the 40-word Note. sTec's "not subject to receipt of financing", line 1578, sits in Reasons, after the June 14 bid. Replacement: "Regulatory and Antitrust may also be filled on Contact, Dropped by target and Withdrew rows where the filing gives regulatory grounds. One page cite covers every code drawn from the row's quoted passage; cite separately only codes drawn elsewhere. A fact from outside the background fills a bid row only if dated no later than that bid; otherwise it goes in the Merger agreement signed Note."

## Works as drafted

- Not stated as non-negative, and Varies on cohort rows.
- One marker for CVR and earnout.
- Highly confident letters as Contingent: "highly confident financing letter from Jefferies", zep line 1530; "highly confident letters from its proposed lenders", meredith line 4090.
- `Not begun` maps to Unclear as E12 already requires; Mac-Gray's July IOIs followed only an "informational package", line 1529.
- `Part stock` for "a combination of cash and stock to be determined", meredith line 3831.
- Required exclusivity codes cleanly: "provided that Mac-Gray agree to a two week period of exclusive negotiations", line 1731.
- Lender, exclusivity period and CVR trigger in the Note; columns blank on Merger agreement signed.
