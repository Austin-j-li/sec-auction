### P-F1 — The final reaffirmation omits the stated financing basis

**Severity: Moderate. Candidates: A and B.**

| Candidate | Sheet and row | Affected value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Deal ledger, row 43 | Financing = Not stated on the 14 October Bid reaffirmed |
| B | Deal ledger, row 48 | Financing = Not stated on the 14 October Bid reaffirmed |

The dated agreement states that Ingredion has sufficient available funds or access under its existing credit agreements as of 14 October.
Source: page A-24, lines 9101–9103, section 4.6: “as of the date of this Agreement immediately available funds”.
The financing summary also states: “There is no financing condition to the merger.” Page 45, lines 4245–4247.
Both candidates record this basis in their execution Notes: A row 46 and B row 51.

Part B, line 28, permits evidence through the next signing row. E12, lines 285–288, requires the reported financing state.
The agreement describes the state as of that day; it does not report a later financing arrangement or change.
Not needed is supported by the existing-facilities rule. Committed is also supported by the express absence of a financing condition.
Not stated omits available evidence for the final offer. This changes one required financing observation, not the price or bidder count.

This finding does not apply the final terms to earlier offers. It applies only to the final 14 October reaffirmation.
The manager should verify the same-day evidence window before using this narrow issue to separate candidates.
