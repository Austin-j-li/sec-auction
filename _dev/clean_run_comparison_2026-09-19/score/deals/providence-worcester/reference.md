- Raw formality comparison: the candidate's label reduced to its head word (Formal / Informal; "non-binding IOI/LOI" with no formal word counts as Informal) against Alex's raw label. Mapped comparison: a candidate label of "Formal with heavy conditions" (or formal + a heavy-conditionality flag) becomes Informal, "Formal with light/no conditions" stays Formal, then compare with the mapped label. Heavy = exclusivity of three weeks or more, a diligence period of four weeks/30 days or more, or a financing contingency; light = "expedited diligence review".

- Dates: exact filing dates must match exactly; "late July" LOIs accepted if assigned July 20 to 22 (3-day tolerance: July 17 to 25); "early July" accepted July 1 to 10.
