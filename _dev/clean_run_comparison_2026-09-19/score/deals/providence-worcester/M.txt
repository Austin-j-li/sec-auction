Commercial account | Initiation was mixed: a Q4 2015 approach by a Class I rail partner (Party A) expressing interest in acquiring equity led, through the Board’s banker search and its 03/14/2016 decision to explore a change of control, to a broad bank-run solicitation of 29 potential buyers (11 strategic, 18 financial) in the week of 03/28/2016. Twenty-five buyers signed confidentiality agreements (no standstills); after introductory meetings and a memorandum, nine submitted non-binding IOIs received 05/19–06/01/2016 at $17.93–$26.50 per share as-converted (equity values $90–134 million), and the committee excluded the two lowest and advanced seven to management presentations and a data site. A mid-June solicitation for LOIs with agreement mark-ups (due 07/20/2016) produced six LOIs in late July at $19.20–$24.00: Party B ($24.00) and G&W ($21.15, then $22.15 including a $1.13 South Quay CVR) led; Party E ($21.26), Party D ($21.00), Party C ($19.30) and Party F ($19.20) followed; one strategic and one financial buyer elected not to submit. On 08/12/2016 G&W’s final $25.00 all-cash LOI (CVR dropped, mark-ups returned, expiry 6:00 p.m. 08/13) displaced Party B’s $24.00 after Party B declined to raise, and the Board approved the merger agreement and the Eder Trusts’ voting agreement that same day. Announcement followed on 08/15/2016; no go-shop, competing post-signing proposal, agreement termination or closing is disclosed, STB approval was pending (petition 09/01/2016; voting trust submission 09/14/2016), and closing was expected in Q4 2016.
