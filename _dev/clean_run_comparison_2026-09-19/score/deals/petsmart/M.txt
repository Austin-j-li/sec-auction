1. STATUS AND COMMERCIAL ACCOUNT
Commercial account | PetSmart's board reviewed strategic alternatives during 2014 after a weak 05/21/2014 first-quarter earnings release, stockholder communications and public activism by JANA Partners (9.9% stake) and Longview; the board's 08/13/2014 decision launched a sale process that was announced on 08/19/2014. Fifteen financial buyers signed confidentiality and standstill agreements in the first week of October and six submitted non-binding indications of interest by 10/30; the four bidders at or above $80.00 advanced to the final round on 11/03, and two of them combined as 'Bidder 3' (bidding units 4 to 3). On 12/12 the Buyer Group submitted a best-and-final $83.00 in cash - after an initial oral $82.50 - Bidder 2 confirmed $81.50 as its best and final, and Longview was approved to work with the Buyer Group on a share rollover. The board approved the merger on 12/13 and PetSmart signed the all-cash $83.00 merger on 12/14/2014 with Argos Holdings (a BC Partners-led consortium that includes Longview from 12/12), with Longview rolling over 3,012,050 shares (about $250 million) and voting 7,424,591 shares (about 7.5%) for the deal. Observed outcome: signing and announcement only - completion was expected in the first half of 2015 and is not observed in the supplied filing; there was no go-shop and no competing or unsolicited proposal reported through the 02/02/2015 proxy date.
Initiation assessment | Mixed - target-led exploration by the board and the ad hoc committee, following activist pressure (JANA, Longview) and inbound interest (Industry Participant's 08/07/2014 approach and 27 contacts), with the 08/19/2014 public announcement deliberately used to widen the field.
