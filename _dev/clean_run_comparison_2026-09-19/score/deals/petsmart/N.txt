Commercial account
Sale story | PetSmart entered 2014 reviewing alternatives — including a possible combination with a privately-held industry participant — and, after a weak first quarter, faced stockholder and activist pressure (JANA Partners' 13D of 07/03/2014 at 9.9%; Longview's public sale advocacy of 07/07/2014) that pushed the board into a formal exploration announced 08/19/2014. Through an independent-director ad hoc committee and J.P. Morgan, the target contacted 27 potential participants, signed confidentiality and standstill agreements with 15 financial buyers in early October, and received six non-binding indications of interest on 10/30/2014 (three individually described $80–$85 ranges and three residual submitters). On 11/03/2014 the board advanced the four bidders at or above $80.00 to a final round; two bidders combined into 'Bidder 3'. Final bids on 12/10/2014 were $80.70 in cash from the Buyer Group and $80.35 from Bidder 2, with Bidder 3 dropping out at a valuation at or below about $78; after the target demanded improvements, Bidder 2 delivered a best-and-final $81.50 and the Buyer Group a best-and-final $83.00 in cash on 12/12/2014. The board approved the Buyer Group on 12/13/2014 and the merger agreement was signed and announced on 12/14/2014: $83.00 per share in cash, with Longview rolling over 3,012,050 shares (~$250 million) into Parent and voting its 7,424,591 shares for the deal. No superior proposal is reported as of the 02/02/2015 proxy; closing was expected in the first half of 2015 and is not observed in the filing.
