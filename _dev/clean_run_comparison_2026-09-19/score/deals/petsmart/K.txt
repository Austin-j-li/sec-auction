The board decided to explore a sale on August 13 and announced it publicly on August 19. In the first week of October, 15 financial buyers signed confidentiality and standstill agreements and received diligence; six submitted non-binding indications of interest by October 30, and on November 3 the board advanced the four bidders at or above $80.00 to a final round, where two of them combined as Bidder 3.
