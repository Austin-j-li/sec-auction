1. Commercial account
Commercial account | Mac-Gray’s Board maintained an ongoing strategic review; after CSC’s May 2013 sale to Pamplona, the Board authorized exploration (04/05/2013), Mac-Gray’s banker approached Party A (04/08/2013), and Party A submitted an unsolicited $17.00–$19.00 all-cash proposal (06/21/2013). On 06/24/2013 the Board formed a six-member Special Committee, excluded CEO MacDonald and Moab’s Rothenberg for rollover conflicts, and launched a solicitation of 50 parties; 20 signed confidentiality agreements (2 strategic, 18 financial) and four bidders — Party A, Party B, Party C and CSC/Pamplona — advanced through staged diligence and revised indications. CSC/Pamplona led the price upward ($18.50 → $19.50 → $20.75 → $21.25 all cash); Party B’s final package was nominally higher at $21.50 but largely contingent options; Party A stood at $18.00–$19.00 best-and-final and Party C dropped out at the final due date. Mac-Gray granted CSC/Pamplona exclusivity on 09/24/2013 and, after resolving the termination-fee dispute, extended it on 10/12/2013 and signed at $21.25 per share in cash on 10/14/2013, announced 10/15/2013; the proxy reports no closing by 12/04/2013 (special meeting 01/08/2014, closing expected January 2014).
Observed outcome | Signed 10/14/2013 with CSC/Pamplona; announced 10/15/2013; no closing observed at the filing date; no post-signing competing proposal or go-shop reported.
