Commercial account
Account | Mac-Gray, an NYSE-listed laundry facilities management company, had reviewed strategic alternatives with BofA Merrill Lynch since April 2013; after Moab's 2013 proxy contest changed the board, a six-member Special Committee launched a sale process on June 24, 2013, approaching 50 parties (15 strategic, 35 financial), of which 20 signed confidentiality agreements. Four bidders advanced — strategic Party A and CSC/Pamplona (CSC ServiceWorks, a Pamplona Capital portfolio company) and financial bidders Party B and Party C. Preliminary indications of $15.00–$19.00 (July) led to staged disclosure and management presentations; revised September indications ranged from $16.00–$17.00 (Party C) to $19.50 (CSC/Pamplona), while Party B offered $19.00 cash plus options it valued at $2.50. In the final round (due September 18), CSC/Pamplona bid $20.75 all cash with committed financing, Party A reiterated $18.00–$19.00 as its best and final, Party B proposed its cash-plus-options package, and Party C did not submit. The Special Committee selected CSC/Pamplona, negotiated a $21.25 all-cash price in exchange for two-week exclusivity from September 24, and signed a definitive agreement on October 14, 2013, announced October 15. No go-shop and no post-signing competing proposal are disclosed; closing was expected in January 2014 / first half of 2014 and is not observed in the filing.
