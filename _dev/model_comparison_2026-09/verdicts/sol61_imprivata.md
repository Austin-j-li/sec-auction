# Verdict: Imprivata / Thoma Bravo (DEFM14A, 08/10/2016; Background pp. 26–37)

## The process as I read it

- Thoma Bravo (TB) approached in early 2015 and June 2015, and again in January 2016, each time with no proposal. On 03/09/2016 it made an unsolicited $15.00 cash indication, "prepared to finance the transaction entirely with equity from its private equity funds", with confirmatory diligence to take 30 days or less (p. 27).
- Goodwin was acting by 03/10/2016 and Barclays was engaged 04/15/2016. ChipLinnemann, LLC is a paid sale consultant (p. 49). On 05/05/2016 the board approved outreach to 15 parties (11 strategic, 4 financial including TB), which ran 05/06–06/09. Seven signed NDAs: 3 strategic and 4 financial, TB on 05/10. One unnamed sponsor declined soon after signing.
- The 06/03 letter set 06/09 as the date for indications. Strategic 1 withdrew 06/08. On 06/09 the bids were Sponsor A $16.50, Sponsor B $17.00–18.00 and TB $17.25 (TB's came with a draft merger agreement, so it is Formal by route 1). Strategic 2 and Strategic 3 did not bid.
- On 06/12 all three sponsors were advanced and Strategic 3 was encouraged to bid; it withdrew 06/14. Sponsor A said it could not improve and left on 06/15, as reported 06/17. Strategic 4 was contacted 06/17 and declined without an NDA.
- The 06/24 "final bid process letters" to Sponsor B and TB set 07/08 as the final bid deadline. On 06/29 Sponsor B said a final bid "would be significantly below" its June 9 price, and after that there were no further discussions. On 07/08 "only Thoma Bravo submitted a bid" ($19.00 cash, diligence complete). On 07/09, after being asked for its best and final offer, TB bid $19.25. The company refused exclusivity on 07/11, and the deal was signed and announced 07/13.

## Ranking

1. **A**: the Sponsor B episode, the round map and the first TB bid's conditions are right, and its three Questions are the real open points. No high- or medium-severity errors.
2. **C**: the entries, prices, NDAs and early exits are correct. But it records Sponsor B's 06/29 hypothetical remark as a Formal final-round bid, drops Sponsor B on 06/30 on a target decision the filing does not report, and adds a round on the strength of Sponsor A's hypothetical 06/15 remark.
3. **B**: it makes the same Sponsor A and Sponsor B choices as C, and also keeps Sponsor B live until signing ("Not selected at signing"), which the filing contradicts.

**Gap A → C: large in consequence, though limited to about four rows.** The disagreements fall on the round count (2 vs 3), the number of Formal bidders in the final round (1 vs 2) and Sponsor B's exit: exactly research uses 1–3. Prices, entries and NDAs agree.

**Gap C → B: small.** B has one extra high-severity exit error. Against that, it records the ChipLinnemann adviser, which C omits.

The checker's "Count blank on Process restarted" warning, raised on all three, is a false positive. Bidder interest is not entry (E3), so the marker closes no participation.
