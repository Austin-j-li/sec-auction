# Verdict: sTec / WDC (DEFM14A, 08/07/2013)

## My reading of the process (from the filing, before judging)

- **Pre-process.** WDC lunches were held in 2011 and summer 2012, with no proposal (earlier approaches). In mid-November 2012 the board decided on a formal review "including exploring a potential sale" (p. 24). Company A's bank approached on 11/14/2012; the meeting was later cancelled. Gibson Dunn is first shown acting on 11/29/2012. Balch Hill's letter of 12/06/2012 urged the board to "explore strategic alternatives": sale is one option. The next dated acquirer contact is Company B on 02/13/2013, 91 days after 11/14/2012, and the special committee was also formed that day. Under E5 that makes two processes, which is a genuine Process Question.
- **Round 1 (03/26/2013).** The board decided to approach strategic buyers, and BofA's outreach began on 04/01, within a week. BofA contacted 18 parties (17 technology companies, 1 sponsor). Six NDAs were signed: E (04/04), D (04/10), F (04/11), G (04/17), the WDC addendum (04/17) and H (05/08). F turned asset-only on 04/24 and E shortly after. Letters on 04/23 and 04/26 asked for IOIs by 05/03. D bid verbally on 04/23 at "greater than $5.60" and in writing on 05/10 at $5.75 cash, with a draft exclusivity agreement. WDC bid $6.60–$7.10 cash on 05/03. G withdrew on 05/03. H bid $5.00–$5.75 on 05/15. The 05/03 outcome is Extended (late bid accepted), because D's bid arrived late and was considered.
- **Round 2 (05/16).** "Final round process letters" and a draft merger agreement went to WDC and D only, due 05/28. H is Dropped by target (inferred, not invited), with exit reason Would not improve (05/23). The filing later calls this a "request for non-binding proposals" (p. 30), so it is Not final under E6. On 05/28 WDC bid $9.15 cash with a mark-up: Formal, route 1. D asked for two weeks.
- **Round 3 (05/29).** Best-and-final requests went out (trigger b). On 05/30 WDC confirmed $9.15, D asked for more time, and the board chose WDC. On 05/31 WDC Withdrew ("not prepared to move forward … at that time"). D was offered more time and then Withdrew on 06/05, so it has no Did-not-submit exit. WDC Re-entered with a bid of $6.60–$7.10 on 06/10, which is Informal. On 06/11 the board demanded a single best price. WDC bid $6.85 on 06/14. The board authorised going ahead on 06/15–16. Shearman sent a draft on 06/20 and then made an undated H3 threat over the don't-ask-don't-waive waiver. The agreement was signed on 06/23 at $6.85 cash and announced on 06/24.

**All three workbooks reproduce this structure almost exactly:**
- two processes, with rounds opened 03/26, 05/16 and 05/29;
- the same Finality, deadline outcomes and exits;
- the same eight bid prices;
- Formality: Formal on 05/28, 05/30 and 06/14, Informal elsewhere;
- auction screen Met with 6 parties, and Initiation target-led.

I checked these shared codings against pp. 24–35 and found them correct. The workbooks differ only in lower-weight rows and notes.

**No workbook has a high- or medium-severity error.** Every bid, price, Stock %, Formality, round and exit date that matters for research is right in all three.

**Checker false positives, shared by all three:**
- The blank Count on Process restarted is correct under E5. Company A never signed an NDA or bid, so the marker closes no participation.
- The one-sided-price warning is wrong. Every Note says "greater than $5.60" or "lower bound".

**Not counted (uncertain):**
- **Regulatory on WDC's last pre-signing bid.** C codes Concern / Antitrust Y, citing the board's weighing of limits on WDC's divestiture obligations and the antitrust end-date extension (pp. 36–37). A and B code Not stated. E12's "divestitures … including a board … weighing that risk" favours C, but the passage sits under "High Likelihood of Completion". C flagged it as R9.
- **Whether Company B is among the 18 contacted.** A and C subtract B from the cohort; B does not. C flags this as R14. Page 36's "17 other potential buyers … directly or through the company's management" makes either reading possible.
- **Exit reason for D's 06/05 withdrawal.** A and C use Other stated reason; B uses Terms or process. Both fit the stated reason: D could not do diligence for more than two weeks.

## Where they differ

1. **Accounting for the 18 contacts.**
   - C gives WDC, E, F, G, A and the sponsor their own Contact rows, bounded by their NDA dates, plus an 8-party cohort. This reconciles to 18.
   - B uses a 15-party cohort holding the parties with no contact row. This also reconciles.
   - A subtracts parties that have no rows, so it does not reconcile.
   - **C is best, B acceptable, A wrong.** Live-bidder counts are unaffected.
2. **WDC's last two communications before signing.**
   - B and C record a Bid reaffirmed on 06/20 (confirmation by documents at $6.85) and a separate H3 Bid dated 06/20–06/23, and flag the choice.
   - A records a single H3 row dated 06/20.
   - **B and C are right.**
3. **Price feedback to H.** B and C have a row; A does not. **B and C are right.**
4. **Company C.**
   - C records its asset-only approach (03/13) and the end of talks (04/15) as rows outside the contest.
   - A and B omit it and say so in a Review item.
   - Both are acceptable; C's version is more informative.
5. **Information-difference rows.**
   - A has a row for the April management presentations. These were offered alike (F declined), so a Note would do.
   - B has a row for the Cost-Reduction Projections given only to WDC on 06/19 (p. 46).
   - C has a row for the diligence calls given only to WDC and D, and notes the projections.
   - All are defensible.
6. **Formality of the 06/14 winning bid.** All three code it Formal (route 2, the 06/11 request inside the best-and-final round). C alone raises it as a Question, with the alternative stated: if round 3 ended at the 05/30 selection, the bid is Informal. A and B treat it as Review items. This is the most research-relevant open reading in the deal, and **C handles it best**.
7. **Other Questions.**
   - A's Q3 (H's exit) applies the instruction's own worked example, so it should be a Review item.
   - B's Q2 (E and F as entrants) and Q4 (WDC's 05/31 withdrawal) are reasonable. E1 and E14 largely decide them, though.

- **B:** usable after light review. Trim the Questions entries and date the 05/31 call as a bound.
- **A:** usable after light review. The bid, round and exit data are sound; correct the contact cohort, add the price-feedback row for H, and re-date or split the 06/20 H3 row before any analysis of contact counts.
