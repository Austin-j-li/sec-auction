# Instruction v1.12: general edits after the eight-deal review

21 September 2026. Austin approved these edits after the audit of the eight v1.11 workbooks and the pipeline research note. He set the governing rule: change the instruction only where the change is general (objective, work process, honesty about uncertainty, a repaired contradiction); never add a rule whose only justification is one reviewed deal. Alex Gorbenko was out of reach, so these are Austin's provisional conventions.

v1.12 is 8,645 words against v1.11's 8,421. The previous record is at `git show 59e2325:_dev/DECISIONS_v1.11.md`; every v1.11 resolution still stands unless listed below.

## Decisions

| Issue found | Change in v1.12 |
| --- | --- |
