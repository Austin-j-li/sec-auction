# Direct prose

Write the requested prose with a calm, definite authorial voice. Let the evidence set the strength of each claim.

Apply this skill to the requested document or passage. For economics or finance manuscripts, read [references/financial-economics.md](references/financial-economics.md).

## Choose the task

- **Draft:** Build the prose from the supplied notes, results, and sources. Arrange them around the intended argument.
- **Revise:** Preserve the author's argument and factual content. Keep local edits local unless the user requests broader changes.
- **Review:** When the user requests comments only, identify substantive prose problems and suggest concrete replacements.

Do not turn a prose task into a research audit, new analysis, or a list of hypothetical referee objections.

## Authorial style

State what the paper finds, argues, estimates, or establishes. Prefer specific subjects, concrete verbs, and stable terminology.

Lead with the paragraph's purpose. Put an assumption or context first when the reader needs it to interpret the claim.

Remove empty openers, apology-like language, repeated disclaimers, and stacked qualifiers that add no meaning. Keep useful contrasts and warranted uncertainty.

Match the supplied author's voice and the intended audience. Use natural academic prose for manuscripts, as the user selected. Do not impose fixed sentence lengths, tense restrictions, or a ban on “-ing” forms unless requested for that document.

Use active or passive voice according to the sentence's purpose. Preserve technical terms, mathematical expressions, and intentional repetition. Do not add quirks or vary precise terms merely to sound human.

## Preserve the claim

Keep numbers, units, populations, dates, comparisons, assumptions, and uncertainty intact. Keep citations attached to the claims they support.

A shorter sentence must retain its original truth conditions. Do not remove a qualification merely because it contains “may,” “suggests,” “only,” or a negative statement.

Place a necessary qualification beside the claim it limits. Repeat it when an abstract, conclusion, caption, or other passage must stand alone. Remove redundant repetition within the same discussion.

Retain unfavorable results that affect interpretation. State their substance directly without apology or an invented explanation.

Do not supply new facts, sources, mechanisms, or advantages to make prose more persuasive. Do not change methods or data treatment during a prose edit.

When the supplied evidence plainly supports a narrower claim, write that claim. Flag any material change in scope or interpretation after the prose. When support is unresolved, use a clearly marked author placeholder or a brief query outside the prose. Ask first only if the missing information blocks a useful draft.

## Deliver the prose

Return the requested text first. Add brief notes only for material changes in claim strength or unresolved support. If the user requests clean copy, return clean copy.

Before delivery, compare the prose with the supplied material. Check for added facts, lost conditions, changed causal language, altered negation, and displaced citations. Leave sound sentences alone.
