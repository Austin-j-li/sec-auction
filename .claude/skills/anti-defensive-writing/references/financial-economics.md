# Financial economics prose

Use these rules for economics and finance manuscripts. Preserve the author's research design; apply the rules to its presentation.

## Match the claim to the evidence

- Distinguish association, causal effects, prediction, mechanism evidence, and welfare implications.
- Distinguish a model proposition, a calibrated result, and an empirical estimate.
- Keep the estimand, sample, horizon, units, benchmark, and treatment definition intact.
- Preserve identification assumptions where the interpretation depends on them. Do not turn a failed rejection into proof of an assumption.
- Keep uncertainty around an estimate. An imprecise estimate differs from a precise estimate near zero.
- Distinguish statistical significance from economic magnitude. Do not replace an insignificant estimate with “no effect.”
- Preserve the role of risk adjustment, costs, and sample selection in return claims. Gross alpha does not establish net profitability.
- Keep local and partial-equilibrium results within their scope. Do not infer aggregate welfare from an isolated response.

State an established observation directly. Explain a proposed mechanism at the strength its evidence supports. Do not automatically weaken a credible causal claim to an association or strengthen an association to causation.

## Preserve technical content

Keep notation, equation labels, cross-references, citation keys, table references, and defined terms intact. Preserve qualifications such as “conditional on,” “within the model,” and “for the compliers” when material.

Use the numbers already supplied to make a result precise. Do not invent magnitudes, uncertainty intervals, institutional details, or comparison groups.

Select the results that serve the requested passage. Do not narrate every table cell. Do not prescribe estimators, robustness work, winsorization, or new comparisons during a prose revision.

## Two boundary examples

An empty opener can disappear without a stronger claim:

- Input: “It is worth noting that leverage is associated with spreads in this sample.”
- Revision: “Leverage is associated with spreads in this sample.”

A substantive negative statement can remain:

- “The pattern is consistent with liquidity relief. The data do not identify the mechanism.”

These examples supply no facts for another document. Use the draft's evidence and terminology.
